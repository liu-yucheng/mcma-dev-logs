Complete the squares: \[ x^2-2x=(x-1)^2-1,\qquad -y^2+2y=-(y-1)^2+1,\qquad z^2+4z=(z+2)^2-4. \] So the equation becomes \[ (x-1)^2-(y-1)^2+(z+2)^2-2=0, \] or, in standard form, \[ \frac{(x-1)^2}{2}+\frac{(z+2)^2}{2}-\frac{(y-1)^2}{2}=1. \] This is a **hyperboloid of one sheet**, centered at \((1,1,-2)\), with its axis parallel to the \(y\)-axis. Cross-sections \(y=\text{constant}\) are circles/ellipses, and the cross-section \(z=-2\) is the hyperbola \((x-1)^2-(y-1)^2=2\). \[ \boxed{\frac{(x-1)^2}{2}+\frac{(z+2)^2}{2}-\frac{(y-1)^2}{2}=1,\ \text{hyperboloid of one sheet, axis parallel to the }y\text{-axis}} \] --- --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./20260825_022650_329295_utc_0800_pgfplots_0.tex)
