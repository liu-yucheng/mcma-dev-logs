Literature Review — Chapter 6

Conclusion and Strategic Recommendations for Future Research

🎯 A math-capable multimodal AI agent with pyplot and PGFplots integration for autonomous tutoring sits at the meeting point of several major research frontiers. The review identifies four significant, actionable research gaps. Such an agent is uniquely placed to fill them.

📋 The four research gaps at a glance

1
📊 The benchmarking gap — no benchmark for generative, iterative, pedagogically-oriented agentic tasks such as calculus surface sketching
2
📄 The PGFplots integration gap — no research on autonomous agents that generate and revise plots with the declarative PGFplots/LaTeX toolchain
3
🧊 The 3D spatial reasoning gap — models cannot reason robustly about 3D geometry; topology errors and 2D/3D confusion
4
🎓 The pedagogical effectiveness gap — the chasm between technical problem-solving and effective teaching
GapWhat is missingRecommendation
Gap 1The benchmarking gap Benchmarks for evaluating generative, iterative, and pedagogically-oriented agentic tasks such as calculus surface sketching. ActPropose and create a new benchmark, “Calculus Sketch.” It would evaluate agents on generating, revising, and pedagogically annotating 3D surfaces and related plots.
Gap 2The PGFplots integration gap Research on autonomous agents that generate and revise plots with the declarative, high-fidelity PGFplots/LaTeX toolchain. ActPublish a foundational paper that details the PGFplots workflow of a math-capable multimodal agent. Conduct a comparative study of the matplotlib vs. PGFplots toolchains for agentic control. Compare strengths, weaknesses, and error modes. This is a wide-open area for high-impact research.
Gap 3The 3D spatial reasoning gap Robust reasoning about 3D geometry: errors in topology, confusion of 2D projections with 3D reality. ActInvestigate and design novel self-correction mechanisms that enforce topological validity in generated surfaces. Explore hybrid architectures. Combine the VLM's language capability with a formal geometry engine to improve spatial reasoning.
Gap 4The pedagogical effectiveness gap The leap from problem-solving to teaching: current models fail to produce pedagogically sound or helpful educational content. ActDevelop a “pedagogical planner” module. It decides what to visualize and how to explain it, based on the calculus concept and a model of the student's understanding. Conduct user studies that compare educational impact against static diagrams or less capable systems.

🧭 Why this is a research platform, not an incremental improvement

By addressing these gaps in order, this line of work can make real contributions to artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and computational education. The agent in this review is more than an incremental improvement. It is a new research platform. With these capabilities, the work can help solve these open problems in AI for education and scientific discovery. It can pave the way for a new generation of capable, effective autonomous tutors.
