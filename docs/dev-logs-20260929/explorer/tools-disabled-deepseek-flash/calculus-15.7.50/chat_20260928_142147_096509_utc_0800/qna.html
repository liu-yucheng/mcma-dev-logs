## Setup of the solid $E$ The cylinder $y^2+z^2=9$ has radius $3$ and axis the $x$-axis. In the first octant, $y\ge 0,\ z\ge 0$, and the plane $y=3x$ gives the remaining bound. The bounded region is therefore $$ E=\left\{(x,y,z):\ 0\le y\le 3,\ 0\le x\le \frac y3,\ 0\le z\le \sqrt{9-y^2}\right\}. $$ The density is $$ \rho(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2. $$ --- ## (a) The mass $$ M=\iiint_E \rho\,dV =\int_0^3\int_0^{y/3}\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}(x^2+y^2)\,dz\,dx\,dy. $$ Integrating in $z$ and then $x$: $$ \begin{aligned} M &=\int_0^3 \sqrt{9-y^2}\left[\frac{(y/3)^3}{3}+y^2\frac y3\right]dy\\ &=\int_0^3 \sqrt{9-y^2}\left[\frac{y^3}{81}+\frac{y^3}{3}\right]dy\\ &=\frac{28}{81}\int_0^3 y^3\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy. \end{aligned} $$ Let $y=3\sin\theta$, $dy=3\cos\theta\,d\theta$, $\sqrt{9-y^2}=3\cos\theta$. Then $$ \int_0^3 y^3\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy =243\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^3\theta\cos^2\theta\,d\theta =243\cdot\frac{2}{15} =\frac{162}{5}. $$ Thus $$ M=\frac{28}{81}\cdot\frac{162}{5}=\frac{56}{5}=11.200. $$ --- ## (b) The center of mass ### $x$-coordinate $$ \begin{aligned} M_x=\iiint_E x\rho\,dV &=\int_0^3\int_0^{y/3}\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}x(x^2+y^2)\,dz\,dx\,dy\\ &=\frac{19}{324}\int_0^3 y^4\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy. \end{aligned} $$ Using $y=3\sin\theta$, $$ \int_0^3 y^4\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy =729\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^4\theta\cos^2\theta\,d\theta =729\cdot\frac{\pi}{32} =\frac{729\pi}{32}. $$ Hence $$ M_x=\frac{19}{324}\cdot\frac{729\pi}{32} =\frac{171\pi}{128}, \qquad \bar x=\frac{M_x}{M} =\frac{171\pi/128}{56/5} =\frac{855\pi}{7168} \approx 0.375. $$ ### $y$-coordinate $$ \begin{aligned} M_y=\iiint_E y\rho\,dV &=\int_0^3\int_0^{y/3}\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}y(x^2+y^2)\,dz\,dx\,dy\\ &=\frac{28}{81}\int_0^3 y^4\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy\\ &=\frac{28}{81}\cdot\frac{729\pi}{32} =\frac{63\pi}{8}. \end{aligned} $$ Therefore $$ \bar y=\frac{M_y}{M} =\frac{63\pi/8}{56/5} =\frac{315\pi}{448} \approx 2.209. $$ ### $z$-coordinate $$ \begin{aligned} M_z=\iiint_E z\rho\,dV &=\int_0^3\int_0^{y/3}\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}z(x^2+y^2)\,dz\,dx\,dy\\ &=\int_0^3\frac{9-y^2}{2}\int_0^{y/3}(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy\\ &=\frac12\cdot\frac{28}{81}\int_0^3 y^3(9-y^2)\,dy\\ &=\frac{14}{81}\left[9\frac{3^4}{4}-\frac{3^6}{6}\right]\\ &=\frac{14}{81}\cdot\frac{243}{4} =\frac{21}{2}. \end{aligned} $$ Thus $$ \bar z=\frac{M_z}{M} =\frac{21/2}{56/5} =\frac{105}{112} =0.9375 \approx 0.938. $$ So the center of mass is approximately $$ (\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)\approx(0.375,\,2.209,\,0.938). $$ --- ## (c) Moment of inertia about the $z$-axis The distance from the $z$-axis is $r^2=x^2+y^2$, so $$ I_z=\iiint_E (x^2+y^2)\rho\,dV =\iiint_E (x^2+y^2)^2\,dV. $$ Integrating first in $z$ and then in $x$: $$ \begin{aligned} I_z &=\int_0^3\int_0^{y/3}\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}(x^4+2x^2y^2+y^4)\,dz\,dx\,dy\\ &=\int_0^3\sqrt{9-y^2}\left[\frac{(y/3)^5}{5}+\frac{2y^2(y/3)^3}{3}+y^4\frac y3\right]dy\\ &=\frac{436}{1215}\int_0^3 y^5\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy. \end{aligned} $$ Now with $y=3\sin\theta$, $$ \int_0^3 y^5\sqrt{9-y^2}\,dy =2187\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^5\theta\cos^2\theta\,d\theta =2187\cdot\frac{8}{105} =\frac{5832}{35}. $$ Therefore $$ I_z=\frac{436}{1215}\cdot\frac{5832}{35} =\frac{10464}{175} \approx 59.7942857 \approx 59.794. $$ --- ## Final answers (rounded to three decimal places) $$ \boxed{\text{Mass }=11.200,\qquad \text{Center of mass }=(0.375,\,2.209,\,0.938),\qquad I_z=59.794} $$ --- No graphical results.
