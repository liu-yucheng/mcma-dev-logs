# Solid enclosed by the paraboloids $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$ and $z=5-x^{2}-y^{2}$ ## 1. What the two surfaces are - $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$ is a **circular paraboloid opening upward** (a "bowl") with vertex at the origin, and its level circles are $z=r^{2}$. - $z=5-x^{2}-y^{2}$ is a **circular paraboloid opening downward** (a "dome") with vertex at $(0,0,5)$, and its level circles are $z=5-r^{2}$. Each surface has the $z$-axis as its axis of symmetry, so it is natural to use **cylindrical coordinates** $x=r\cos\theta,\; y=r\sin\theta,\; z=z$. ## 2. Where the two surfaces meet Set the two heights equal: $$ x^{2}+y^{2}=5-x^{2}-y^{2}\;\Longrightarrow\;2\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)=5\;\Longrightarrow\;x^{2}+y^{2}=\tfrac{5}{2}. $$ So they intersect exactly on the **circle** $$ r=\sqrt{\tfrac{5}{2}}\approx 1.5811,\qquad z=\tfrac{5}{2}=2.5, $$ centered on the $z$-axis at height $z=2.5$. This circle is the "equator" (the sharp edge) of the solid. ## 3. The enclosed region For every point with $r^{2}\le \tfrac{5}{2}$ the dome lies **above** the bowl, because $$ 5-r^{2}\ \ge\ r^{2}\iff r^{2}\le \tfrac{5}{2}. $$ Hence the enclosed solid is the "lens / lemon" trapped between the two paraboloids: it is bounded **below** by $z=r^{2}$ and **above** by $z=5-r^{2}$. --- Image `pyplot_0.png` not found. Reading the picture - The **lower (blue) sheet** is $z=x^{2}+y^{2}$; the **upper (orange) sheet** is $z=5-x^{2}-y^{2}$. - Both sheets are drawn only over the disc $0\le r\le\sqrt{5/2}$, so they close up into a single solid whose only "edge" is the black **intersection circle** at $z=5/2$. - The solid is symmetric about the plane $z=5/2$ (swapping the two paraboloids maps the solid to itself) and is a surface of revolution about the $z$-axis. ## 5. Cylindrical description of the solid $$ \boxed{\;\mathcal{S}=\Bigl\{(x,y,z):\;x^{2}+y^{2}\le z\le 5-x^{2}-y^{2}\Bigr\} =\Bigl\{(r,\theta,z):\;0\le r\le\sqrt{\tfrac{5}{2}},\;0\le\theta\le 2\pi,\;r^{2}\le z\le 5-r^{2}\Bigr\}\;} $$ That is: the solid is the region **inside the bowl and under the dome**, i.e. between the two paraboloids, extending out to the circle $r=\sqrt{5/2}$. ### How to draw it (method) 1. Recognize both graphs are paraboloids of revolution about the $z$-axis. 2. Solve for the intersection: $r^{2}=5/2$ gives the rim circle at height $z=5/2$. 3. Sketch both surfaces **restricted to $r\le\sqrt{5/2}$** (if you sketch them over a larger $xy$-domain they cross and the picture becomes cluttered); their union is the closed boundary of the solid. 4. Shade the solid between them (or draw the two translucent sheets plus the rim circle), as in the figure above. *(Optional check.* Using the description above, its volume is $V=\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2\pi}\!\!\int_{0}^{\sqrt{5/2}}\!\!(5-2r^{2})\,r\,dr\,d\theta=\tfrac{25\pi}{4}\approx 19.63$, a useful consistency test of the drawing.)
