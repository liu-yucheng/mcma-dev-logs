## Setting up the integrals **Solid, density:** $\;\rho(x,y,z)=\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}$. ### 1. Description of the solid region The solid is bounded by three surfaces: - the **parabolic cylinder** $y=x^{2}$ (extruded parallel to the $z$-axis), - the plane $z=0$ (bottom), and - the plane $y+z=1$, i.e. $z=1-y$ (top). The two planes meet where $z=0$ and $y=1$, along the line $y=1$. Take a cross-section at a fixed $x$ (viewed in the $yz$-plane): the cylinder becomes the vertical line $y=x^{2}$, the bottom is $z=0$, and the top is $z=1-y$. These three lines bound a triangle exactly when $x^{2}\le 1$. Hence the solid is $$ E=\{(x,y,z):\ -1\le x\le 1,\quad x^{2}\le y\le 1,\quad 0\le z\le 1-y\}, $$ whose projection on the $xy$-plane is the parabolic region $D:\ -1\le x\le 1,\ x^{2}\le y\le 1$. *Check of the volume.* $\;V=\iint_{D}(1-y)\,dA=\int_{-1}^{1}\Big[(1-y)\,\Big|_{x^{2}}^{1}\Big]dx=\int_{-1}^{1}\Big(\tfrac12-x^{2}+\tfrac{x^{4}}{2}\Big)dx=\tfrac{8}{15}.$ ### (a) Mass $$ m=\iiint_{E}\rho\,dV=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y}\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\;dz\,dy\,dx. $$ ### (b) Center of mass The center of mass is $(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)=\dfrac{1}{m}\big(M_{yz},\,M_{xz},\,M_{xy}\big)$, where $M_{yz}=\iiint x\rho\,dV$, etc. **Symmetry.** The region $E$ is invariant under $x\mapsto -x$, and $\rho$ is even in $x$. Therefore the integrand $x\rho$ is odd in $x$, so $M_{yz}=0$ and $$ \bar x=0. $$ The other two coordinates are $$ \bar y=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y} y\,\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\;dz\,dy\,dx, \qquad \bar z=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y} z\,\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\;dz\,dy\,dx. $$ ### (c) Moment of inertia about the $z$-axis The squared distance from a point to the $z$-axis is $x^{2}+y^{2}$, so $$ I_{z}=\iiint_{E}(x^{2}+y^{2})\,\rho\,dV=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y}(x^{2}+y^{2})^{3/2}\;dz\,dy\,dx. $$ ### 2. Simplification by doing the (trivial) $z$-integration Only the limits and the factor $(1-y)$ depend on $z$, so the inner integrals collapse: $$ m=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}(1-y)\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,dy\,dx, $$ $$ \bar y=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1} y\,(1-y)\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,dy\,dx, \qquad \bar z=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\frac{(1-y)^{2}}{2}\,\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,dy\,dx, $$ $$ I_{z}=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}(1-y)\,(x^{2}+y^{2})^{3/2}\,dy\,dx. $$ **Boxed result (set up, not evaluated):** $$ \boxed{ \begin{aligned} m&=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y}\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\;dz\,dy\,dx,\\[4pt] \bar x&=0,\quad \bar y=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y} y\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,dz\,dy\,dx,\quad \bar z=\frac{1}{m}\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y} z\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\,dz\,dy\,dx,\\[4pt] I_{z}&=\int_{-1}^{1}\!\!\int_{x^{2}}^{1}\!\!\int_{0}^{1-y}(x^{2}+y^{2})^{3/2}\,dz\,dy\,dx. \end{aligned}} $$ --- Image `pyplot_0.png` not found.
