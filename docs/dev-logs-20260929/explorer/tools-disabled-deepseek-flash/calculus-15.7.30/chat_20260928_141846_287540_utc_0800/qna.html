The solid is the right circular cylinder of radius $3$ whose axis is the $x$-axis, between the planes $x=-2$ and $x=2$. Thus $$ E=\{(x,y,z):-2\le x\le 2,\; y^2+z^2\le 9\}. $$ Its projection onto the $yz$-plane is the disk $D:\,y^2+z^2\le 9$. For fixed $y$, the $z$-limits are $\pm\sqrt{9-y^2}$; for fixed $z$, the $y$-limits are $\pm\sqrt{9-z^2}$; and the $x$-limits are always $-2\le x\le 2$. Using these observations, the six possible orders of integration are: 1. $dx\,dy\,dz$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}\int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz $$ 2. $dx\,dz\,dy$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}\int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy $$ 3. $dy\,dx\,dz$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dx\,dz $$ 4. $dy\,dz\,dx$: $$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dz\,dx $$ 5. $dz\,dx\,dy$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dx\,dy $$ 6. $dz\,dy\,dx$: $$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx $$ As a check, when $f=1$, the first integral gives $$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}2\sqrt{9-y^{2}}\,dy\,dx =4\cdot 9\pi=36\pi, $$ the volume of the cylinder. $$ \boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}\int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz,\\[2mm] &\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}\int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy,\\[2mm] &\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dx\,dz,\\[2mm] &\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-z^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dz\,dx,\\[2mm] &\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dx\,dy,\\[2mm] &\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx. \end{aligned} } $$ --- No graphical results.
