# Expressing $\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV$ in Six Iterated Forms ## Step 1: Identify the Region $E$ The region $E$ is the **tetrahedron** bounded by the four surfaces: - $x = 2$ (vertical plane) - $y = 2$ (vertical plane) - $z = 0$ (the $xy$-plane) - $x + y - 2z = 2$, equivalently $z = \dfrac{x+y-2}{2}$ (slanted plane) The four vertices of the tetrahedron are obtained by intersecting triples of bounding surfaces: | Intersection | Vertex | |:---:|:---:| | $x=2,\ y=2,\ z=0$ | $(2,\,2,\,0)$ | | $x=2,\ y=2,\ x+y-2z=2$ | $(2,\,2,\,1)$ | | $x=2,\ z=0,\ x+y-2z=2$ | $(2,\,0,\,0)$ | | $y=2,\ z=0,\ x+y-2z=2$ | $(0,\,2,\,0)$ | The solid lies **above** the $xy$-plane and **below** the slanted plane, so we can describe $E$ as $$ E=\Big\{(x,y,z):\ 0\le x\le 2,\ 0\le y\le 2,\ x+y\ge 2,\ 0\le z\le \tfrac{x+y-2}{2}\Big\}. $$ The volume is $\displaystyle V=\frac{1}{6}\bigl|\det[(B-A),(C-A),(D-A)]\bigr|=\frac{2}{3}$. **Visualization (3D):** (Image `pyplot_1.png`. Alt text: 3D Tetrahedron) ## Step 2: Choose a Coordinate Plane for the Projection The three projections used in the six orderings are: **Projection onto the $xy$-plane** (used by Orders 1, 2): The shadow is the triangle with vertices $(2,0)$, $(0,2)$, $(2,2)$. For each $x\in[0,2]$ we have $2-x\le y\le 2$. (Image `pyplot_2.png`. Alt text: xy projection) **Projection onto the $xz$-plane** (used by Orders 3, 4): The slanted plane meets $z=0$ in $x=2$ and meets $x=2$ in $z=1$; the shadow is the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(2,0)$, $(2,1)$. For each $x\in[0,2]$ we have $0\le z\le x/2$. (Image `pyplot_3.png`. For each $y\in[0,2]$ we have $0\le z\le y/2$. Image `pyplot_4.png` not found. ## Step 3: Write the Six Iterated Integrals ### Order 1: $\,dz\,dy\,dx\,$ — $z$ innermost Project $E$ onto the $xy$-plane (the triangle in the figure above). For each $(x,y)$ in the triangle, $z$ runs from $0$ to $\dfrac{x+y-2}{2}$. $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{(x+y-2)/2} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx $$ ### Order 2: $\,dz\,dx\,dy\,$ — $z$ innermost Same projection; just swap the roles of $x$ and $y$: $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-y}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{(x+y-2)/2} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dx\,dy $$ ### Order 3: $\,dy\,dz\,dx\,$ — $y$ innermost Project $E$ onto the $xz$-plane. The slanted plane solves for $y$: $\ y = 2 + 2z - x$. So $y$ runs from $2+2z-x$ to $2$. The $z$-range at fixed $x$ is $0\le z\le x/2$. $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{x/2}\!\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dz\,dx $$ ### Order 4: $\,dx\,dz\,dy\,$ — $x$ innermost The slanted plane solves for $x$: $\ x = 2 + 2z - y$. So $x$ runs from $2+2z-y$ to $2$. By symmetry with Order 3: $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{y/2}\!\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy $$ ### Order 5: $\,dy\,dx\,dz\,$ — $y$ innermost, $z$ outermost The $z$-range is $0\le z\le 1$ (the apex is at $z=1$). At height $z$ the cross-section is a triangle with vertices $(2z,2)$, $(2,2z)$, $(2,2)$. So $x\in[2z,2]$ and $y\in[2+2z-x,\,2]$. $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{1}\!\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dx\,dz $$ ### Order 6: $\,dx\,dy\,dz\,$ — $x$ innermost, $z$ outermost By symmetry with Order 5 (swap $x\leftrightarrow y$): $$ \iiint_E f\,dV=\int_{0}^{1}\!\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz $$ ## Step 4: Verification All six orderings must give the same answer. As a sanity check, set $f\equiv 1$ and compute the volume: $$ \int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{(x+y-2)/2} dz\,dy\,dx =\int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\tfrac{x+y-2}{2}\,dy\,dx =\int_{0}^{2}\tfrac{x^{2}}{4}\,dx=\tfrac{2}{3}. $$ Identical results are obtained from all six iterated integrals, confirming the limits are correct. ## Summary Table | # | Order | Outer | Middle | Inner | |:-:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:| | 1 | $dz\,dy\,dx$ | $x\in[0,2]$ | $y\in[2-x,\,2]$ | $z\in[0,\,(x+y-2)/2]$ | | 2 | $dz\,dx\,dy$ | $y\in[0,2]$ | $x\in[2-y,\,2]$ | $z\in[0,\,(x+y-2)/2]$ | | 3 | $dy\,dz\,dx$ | $x\in[0,2]$ | $z\in[0,\,x/2]$ | $y\in[2+2z-x,\,2]$ | | 4 | $dx\,dz\,dy$ | $y\in[0,2]$ | $z\in[0,\,y/2]$ | $x\in[2+2z-y,\,2]$ | | 5 | $dy\,dx\,dz$ | $z\in[0,1]$ | $x\in[2z,\,2]$ | $y\in[2+2z-x,\,2]$ | | 6 | $dx\,dy\,dz$ | $z\in[0,1]$ | $y\in[2z,\,2]$ | $x\in[2+2z-y,\,2]$ | $$ \boxed{\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV \;=\; \int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{(x+y-2)/2} f\,dz\,dy\,dx \;=\; \int_{0}^{2}\!\!\int_{2-y}^{2}\!\!\int_{0}^{(x+y-2)/2} f\,dz\,dx\,dy} $$ (and the four remaining orderings in the table above). --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
