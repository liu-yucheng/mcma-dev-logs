## Setting Up the Region The region $E$ lies in the first octant and is bounded by four surfaces: - **Cylinder** $y^2+z^2 = 9$ (with axis along the $x$-axis, radius $3$) - **Plane** $x = 0$ (the $yz$-plane) - **Plane** $y = 3x$ (a vertical plane through the $x$-axis) - **Plane** $z = 0$ (the $xy$-plane) For the region to be **bounded** in the first octant, the plane $y = 3x$ must act as the upper bound on $x$, giving $x \le y/3$. (Choosing the other side would leave $x$ unbounded.) The cylinder and $z=0$ together bound $y$ and $z$ inside a quarter disk. Consequently: $$ E = \left\{(x,y,z): 0 \le x \le \tfrac{y}{3},\ \ 0 \le y \le 3,\ \ 0 \le z \le \sqrt{9-y^2}\right\} $$ The two key 2D projections are shown below. Image `pyplot_2.png` not found. - The $yz$-projection is the **quarter disk** $y^2+z^2\le 9$, $y,z\ge 0$. - The $xy$-projection is the **wedge** $\{(x,y):0\le x\le y/3,\ 0\le y\le 3\}$, meeting the line $y=3x$ at the corner $(1,3)$. A 3D view of the region is shown below. Image `pyplot_3.png` not found. ## Evaluating the Triple Integral Choose the order $\,dx\,dz\,dy$: $$ \iiint_E z\,dV = \int_{0}^{3}\!\!\int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}}\!\!\int_{0}^{y/3} z\,dx\,dz\,dy. $$ **Step 1 — integrate with respect to $x$:** $$ \int_{0}^{y/3} z\,dx = z\cdot\frac{y}{3} = \frac{yz}{3}. $$ **Step 2 — integrate with respect to $z$:** $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} \frac{yz}{3}\,dz = \frac{y}{3}\cdot\frac{z^{2}}{2}\Big|_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} = \frac{y}{6}\bigl(9-y^{2}\bigr). $$ **Step 3 — integrate with respect to $y$:** $$ \int_{0}^{3} \frac{y(9-y^{2})}{6}\,dy = \frac{1}{6}\int_{0}^{3}(9y-y^{3})\,dy = \frac{1}{6}\left[\frac{9y^{2}}{2}-\frac{y^{4}}{4}\right]_{0}^{3}. $$ Substituting $y=3$: $$ = \frac{1}{6}\left[\frac{9(9)}{2}-\frac{81}{4}\right] = \frac{1}{6}\left[\frac{81}{2}-\frac{81}{4}\right] = \frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{81}{4} = \frac{81}{24}. $$ Simplifying: $$ \boxed{\,\iiint_E z\,dV = \dfrac{27}{8}\,}. $$ ## Verification A Monte-Carlo check with $10^{6}$ sample points in the bounding box yields $$ \iiint_E z\,dV \approx 3.3751218 \approx \tfrac{27}{8}, $$ and re-doing the integral in the order $dy\,dz\,dx$ (with bounds $x\in[0,1]$, $z\in[0,3\sqrt{1-x^2}]$, $y\in[3x,\sqrt{9-z^2}]$) gives the same value $\frac{27}{8}$. The region's volume is $$ V = \int_0^3\!\!\int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}\!\!\int_0^{y/3} dx\,dz\,dy = 3, $$ so the average value of $z$ on $E$ is $\dfrac{27/8}{3} = \dfrac{9}{8}$. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
