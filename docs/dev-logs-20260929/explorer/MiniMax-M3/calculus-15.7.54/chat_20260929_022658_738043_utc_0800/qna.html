## Setting Up the Problem The function $f(x,y,z) = x^2 z + y^2 z$ must be averaged over the solid region $E$ bounded below by the plane $z = 0$ and above by the paraboloid $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$. Setting them equal gives $x^2 + y^2 = 1$, so the projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane is the unit disk. **Strategy:** Use cylindrical coordinates. Then $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$, so $f = r^2 z$, and $dV = r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta$. $$ f_{\text{ave}} = \frac{1}{V(E)}\iiint_E f\,dV $$ ## Step 1 — Volume of $E$ $$ V(E) = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^{1-r^2} r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta = 2\pi \int_0^1 r(1-r^2)\,dr = 2\pi\!\left[\tfrac{r^2}{2}-\tfrac{r^4}{4}\right]_0^1 = \frac{\pi}{2} $$ ## Step 2 — Triple Integral $$ \iiint_E (x^2z+y^2z)\,dV = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1\!\!\int_0^{1-r^2} r^2 z \cdot r\,dz\,dr\,d\theta $$ $$ = 2\pi \int_0^1 r^3 \cdot \frac{(1-r^2)^2}{2}\,dr = \pi \int_0^1 \bigl(r^3 - 2r^5 + r^7\bigr)\,dr = \pi\!\left[\tfrac{r^4}{4}-\tfrac{r^6}{3}+\tfrac{r^8}{8}\right]_0^1 $$ $$ = \pi\!\left(\tfrac{1}{4}-\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{1}{8}\right) = \pi\cdot\frac{6-8+3}{24} = \frac{\pi}{24} $$ ## Step 3 — Average Value $$ f_{\text{ave}} = \frac{\pi/24}{\pi/2} = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{12}} $$ (Sympy verification gives $V(E)=\pi/2$, the integral $= \pi/24$, and the ratio $= 1/12$.) ## Geometric Interpretation The figure on the right shows the cross-section of $E$ in the $rz$-plane (rotation about the $z$-axis gives $E$). The solid blue region under $z=1-r^2$ is $E$ in profile. The dashed green horizontal line at $z = 1/12$ represents $f_{\text{ave}}$ — the constant height such that a rectangular slab of that height over the unit disk would have the same total integral as $f(x,y,z)$ over $E$. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
