## Setting up the region The solid $E$ lies in the first octant and is bounded by the cylinder $y^2+z^2=9$, the planes $x=0$, $y=3x$, and $z=0$. The plane $y=3x$ together with $x=0$ and $z=0$ forms a wedge in the first octant whose edge is the $x$-axis. The cylinder caps this wedge where $y^2+z^2=9$. **Coordinate choice.** Because the cylinder has its axis along the $x$-axis, use polar coordinates in the $yz$-plane: $$y=r\cos\theta,\qquad z=r\sin\theta,\qquad r\in[0,3],\ \ \theta\in[0,\pi/2].$$ For a fixed $(r,\theta)$ on the quarter disk, $x$ runs from $0$ (plane $x=0$) to $y/3=r\cos\theta/3$ (plane $y=3x$). The Jacobian is $r$. So the integral element is $dV=r\,dx\,dr\,d\theta$ and the density becomes $$\rho(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2=x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta.$$ Image `pyplot_0` not found. The wedge shape (right of the $y=3x$ line in the $xy$-projection) makes it natural to integrate $x$ first, then $r$, then $\theta$. --- ## (a) Mass $$M=\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\!\int_0^{3}\!\!\int_0^{r\cos\theta/3}(x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta)\,r\,dx\,dr\,d\theta.$$ **Inner integral:** $$\int_0^{r\cos\theta/3}(x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta)\,dx=\frac{r^3\cos^3\theta}{81}+\frac{r^3\cos^3\theta}{3}=\frac{28\,r^3\cos^3\theta}{81}.$$ **After multiplying by $r$ and integrating over $r$:** $$\int_0^3\frac{28\,r^4\cos^3\theta}{81}\,dr=\frac{28\cos^3\theta}{81}\cdot\frac{243}{5}=\frac{84\cos^3\theta}{5}.$$ **Outer integral** using $\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^3\theta\,d\theta=2/3$: $$M=\frac{84}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3}=\boxed{\frac{56}{5}\approx 11.200}.$$ --- ## (b) Center of mass Use $\bar x=\dfrac{M_{yz}}{M}$, $\bar y=\dfrac{M_{xz}}{M}$, $\bar z=\dfrac{M_{xy}}{M}$ where each moment is $\iiint (\text{coord})\,\rho\,dV$. **For $\bar x$:** $\int_0^{r\cos\theta/3}x(x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta)\,dx=\dfrac{r^4\cos^4\theta}{324}+\dfrac{r^4\cos^4\theta}{18}=\dfrac{19\,r^4\cos^4\theta}{324}.$ Multiply by $r$ and integrate $r$: $\dfrac{19\cos^4\theta}{324}\cdot\dfrac{729}{6}=\dfrac{57\cos^4\theta}{8}.$ Then $M_{yz}=\dfrac{57}{8}\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^4\theta\,d\theta=\dfrac{57}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3\pi}{16}=\dfrac{171\pi}{128}.$ $$\bar x=\frac{171\pi/128}{56/5}=\frac{855\pi}{7168}\approx 0.375.$$ **For $\bar y$:** The integrand is just $r\cos\theta$ times the mass inner-integrand: $$M_{xz}=\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\!\int_0^3\frac{28\,r^5\cos^4\theta}{81}\,dr\,d\theta=\int_0^{\pi/2}42\cos^4\theta\,d\theta=42\cdot\frac{3\pi}{16}=\frac{63\pi}{8}.$$ $$\bar y=\frac{63\pi/8}{56/5}=\frac{45\pi}{64}\approx 2.209.$$ **For $\bar z$:** $M_{xy}=\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\!\int_0^3\frac{28\,r^5\sin\theta\cos^3\theta}{81}\,dr\,d\theta=\int_0^{\pi/2}42\sin\theta\cos^3\theta\,d\theta=42\cdot\frac{1}{4}=\frac{21}{2}.$ $$\bar z=\frac{21/2}{56/5}=\frac{15}{16}\approx 0.938.$$ $$\boxed{(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)\approx(0.375,\ 2.209,\ 0.938).}$$ Image `pyplot_1` not found. The center of mass lies on the slanted face of the wedge (as expected by symmetry of the density in $x^2$ and the asymmetry from the $y=3x$ cut). --- ## (c) Moment of inertia about the z-axis $$I_z=\iiint(x^2+y^2)\,\rho\,dV=\int_0^{\pi/2}\!\!\int_0^3\!\!\int_0^{r\cos\theta/3}(x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta)^2\,r\,dx\,dr\,d\theta.$$ **Inner integral** with $u=r\cos\theta/3$: $$\int_0^u(x^2+r^2\cos^2\theta)^2\,dx=\frac{u^5}{5}+\frac{2r^2\cos^2\theta\,u^3}{3}+r^4\cos^4\theta\,u.$$ Substituting $u=r\cos\theta/3$ and simplifying, $$=\frac{r^5\cos^5\theta}{1215}+\frac{2r^5\cos^5\theta}{81}+\frac{r^5\cos^5\theta}{3}=\frac{436\,r^5\cos^5\theta}{1215}.$$ **After multiplying by $r$ and integrating over $r$:** $$\int_0^3\frac{436\,r^6\cos^5\theta}{1215}\,dr=\frac{436\cos^5\theta}{1215}\cdot\frac{2187}{7}=\frac{3924\cos^5\theta}{35}.$$ **Outer integral** using $\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^5\theta\,d\theta=8/15$: $$I_z=\frac{3924}{35}\cdot\frac{8}{15}=\frac{31392}{525}=\boxed{\frac{10464}{175}\approx 59.794}.$$ --- ## Summary of answers | Quantity | Exact value | Decimal (3 dp) | |---|---|---| | Mass $M$ | $56/5$ | $11.200$ | | $\bar x$ | $855\pi/7168$ | $0.375$ | | $\bar y$ | $45\pi/64$ | $2.209$ | | $\bar z$ | $15/16$ | $0.938$ | | $I_z$ | $10464/175$ | $59.794$ | $$\boxed{\,M=11.200,\quad(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)=(0.375,\,2.209,\,0.938),\quad I_z=59.794.\,}$$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
