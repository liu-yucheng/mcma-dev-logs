## Drawing a Silo: Cylinder Surmounted by a Hemisphere The silo is composed of two solids stacked along the positive $z$-axis: 1. **Cylinder**: radius $R=3$, height $H=10$, occupying $$x^2+y^2\le 9,\qquad 0\le z\le 10.$$ 2. **Hemisphere**: same radius $R=3$, centered at $(0,0,H)=(0,0,10)$, occupying $$x^2+y^2+(z-10)^2\le 9,\qquad z\ge 10.$$ So the silo extends from $z=0$ up to the apex at $z=H+R=13$. ### Parametrizations used * **Cylinder** (lateral surface): $(x,y,z)=(3\cos\theta,\,3\sin\theta,\,z)$ with $0\le\theta\le 2\pi$, $0\le z\le 10$. * **Hemisphere**: $(x,y,z)=(3\sin\varphi\cos\theta,\,3\sin\varphi\sin\theta,\,10+3\cos\varphi)$ with $0\le\theta\le 2\pi$, $0\le\varphi\le \pi/2$. The resulting drawing (rendered below with both a `pyplot` 3D plot and a `PGFplots`/tikz 3D plot) shows the tall cylinder capped by the smooth hemispherical dome — exactly the shape of a grain silo. The base circle at $z=0$, the junction circle at $z=10$, and the apex at $z=13$ are clearly visible, with vertical edges and meridians added for clarity. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
