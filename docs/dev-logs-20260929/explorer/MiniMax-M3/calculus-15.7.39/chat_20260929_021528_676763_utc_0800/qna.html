## Setting Up the Region The solid $E$ lies under the plane $z = 1+x+y$ and above the region $D$ in the $xy$-plane bounded by $y = \sqrt{x}$, $y = 0$, and $x = 1$. The base region $D$ is best described with $x$ outer and $y$ inner: $$D = \{(x,y) : 0 \le x \le 1,\; 0 \le y \le \sqrt{x}\}$$ (Image `pyplot_1.png`. Alt text: Region D in xy-plane) So the solid is $$E = \{(x,y,z) : 0 \le x \le 1,\; 0 \le y \le \sqrt{x},\; 0 \le z \le 1+x+y\}$$ with constant density $\rho(x,y,z) = 2$. ## Computing the Mass $$ m = \iiint_E \rho\, dV = 2\int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}}\!\int_0^{1+x+y} dz\, dy\, dx $$ **Inner integral in $z$:** $$ \int_0^{1+x+y} dz = 1 + x + y $$ **Next integral in $y$:** $$ \int_0^{\sqrt{x}} (1+x+y)\,dy = (1+x)\sqrt{x} + \tfrac{x}{2} $$ **Outer integral in $x$:** $$ \int_0^1\!\left[(1+x)\sqrt{x} + \tfrac{x}{2}\right] dx = \int_0^1\!\left(x^{1/2} + x^{3/2} + \tfrac{x}{2}\right) dx = \tfrac{2}{3} + \tfrac{2}{5} + \tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{40+24+15}{60} = \tfrac{79}{60} $$ Therefore $$ \boxed{\,m = 2 \cdot \tfrac{79}{60} = \tfrac{79}{30}\,} $$ ## Computing the Moments ### Moment $M_{yz}$ (for $\bar{x}$) $$ M_{yz} = 2\int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}}\!\int_0^{1+x+y}\! x\, dz\, dy\, dx = 2\int_0^1 x\!\left[(1+x)\sqrt{x} + \tfrac{x}{2}\right] dx $$ $$ = 2\int_0^1\!\left(x^{3/2} + x^{5/2} + \tfrac{x^2}{2}\right) dx = 2\!\left(\tfrac{2}{5} + \tfrac{2}{7} + \tfrac{1}{6}\right) = 2 \cdot \tfrac{179}{210} = \tfrac{179}{105} $$ ### Moment $M_{xz}$ (for $\bar{y}$) $$ M_{xz} = 2\int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}}\!\int_0^{1+x+y}\! y\, dz\, dy\, dx = 2\int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}} y(1+x+y)\, dy\, dx $$ Inner: $$ \int_0^{\sqrt{x}} y(1+x+y)\, dy = \tfrac{x+x^2}{2} + \tfrac{x^{3/2}}{3} $$ Outer: $$ 2\int_0^1\!\left(\tfrac{x+x^2}{2} + \tfrac{x^{3/2}}{3}\right) dx = 2\!\left(\tfrac{1}{4} + \tfrac{1}{6} + \tfrac{2}{15}\right) = 2 \cdot \tfrac{11}{20} = \tfrac{11}{10} $$ ### Moment $M_{xy}$ (for $\bar{z}$) $$ M_{xy} = 2\int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}}\!\int_0^{1+x+y}\! z\, dz\, dy\, dx = \int_0^1\!\int_0^{\sqrt{x}} (1+x+y)^2\, dy\, dx $$ Expanding $(1+x+y)^2 = 1 + 2x + 2y + x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ and integrating in $y$: $$ \int_0^{\sqrt{x}} (1+x+y)^2 dy = (1+x)^2\sqrt{x} + x + x^2 + \tfrac{x^{3/2}}{3} $$ Then in $x$: $$ \int_0^1 \!\left[(1+x)^2\sqrt{x} + x + x^2 + \tfrac{x^{3/2}}{3}\right] dx = \int_0^1 \!\left(x^{1/2} + 2x^{3/2} + x^{5/2} + x + x^2 + \tfrac{x^{3/2}}{3}\right) dx $$ $$ = \tfrac{2}{3} + \tfrac{4}{5} + \tfrac{2}{7} + \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{2}{15} = \tfrac{571}{210} $$ ## Center of Mass $$ \bar{x} = \frac{M_{yz}}{m} = \frac{179/105}{79/30} = \frac{179 \cdot 30}{105 \cdot 79} = \frac{179 \cdot 2}{7 \cdot 79} = \frac{358}{553} $$ $$ \bar{y} = \frac{M_{xz}}{m} = \frac{11/10}{79/30} = \frac{11 \cdot 30}{10 \cdot 79} = \frac{33}{79} $$ $$ \bar{z} = \frac{M_{xy}}{m} = \frac{571/210}{79/30} = \frac{571 \cdot 30}{210 \cdot 79} = \frac{571}{7 \cdot 79} = \frac{571}{553} $$ Image `pgfplots_2.png` not found. The 3D figure shows the solid capped by the tilted plane $z=1+x+y$, with the center of mass marked in red at approximately $(0.647,\, 0.418,\, 1.033)$. ## Final Answer $$ \boxed{\,m = \dfrac{79}{30},\qquad (\bar x,\bar y,\bar z) = \left(\dfrac{358}{553},\; \dfrac{33}{79},\; \dfrac{571}{553}\right)\,} $$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
