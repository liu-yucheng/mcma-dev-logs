## Setting up the region $E$ The solid is trapped between the parabolic cylinder $z=1-y^2$, the slanted plane $x+z=1$, the back plane $x=0$, and the floor $z=0$. Because $z=1-y^2\ge 0$ forces $-1\le y\le 1$, and for each $(y,z)$ the plane $x+z=1$ caps $x$ at $1-z$, we may iterate in the order $dx\,dz\,dy$: $$E=\Big\{(x,y,z):\;-1\le y\le 1,\quad 0\le z\le 1-y^2,\quad 0\le x\le 1-z\Big\}.$$ The plots below show the solid and its two relevant coordinate-plane projections, which are exactly the shadow regions we integrate over. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)

--- ## Step 1 — Mass With the constant density $\rho=4$, the mass is $4$ times the volume: $$m=\iiint_E \rho\,dV=4\iiint_E dV.$$ **Volume.** Integrate $x$ first: $$V=\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}\underbrace{\int_{0}^{1-z}dx}_{=1-z}\,dz\,dy =\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}(1-z)\,dz\,dy.$$ The $z$-integral is $$\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}(1-z)\,dz=\Big[z-\tfrac{z^{2}}{2}\Big]_{0}^{1-y^{2}}=(1-y^{2})-\tfrac{(1-y^{2})^{2}}{2}.$$ Now use the standard even-function integrals $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})\,dy=\tfrac{4}{3}$ and $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})^{2}\,dy=\tfrac{16}{15}$: $$V=\frac{4}{3}-\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{16}{15}=\frac{4}{3}-\frac{8}{15}=\frac{20-8}{15}=\frac{12}{15}=\frac{4}{5}.$$ Therefore $$\boxed{m=4\cdot\frac{4}{5}=\frac{16}{5}}.$$ --- ## Step 2 — Center of mass With constant density the centroid formula is $\bar{x}=\dfrac{1}{V}\displaystyle\iiint_E x\,dV$, etc. **Symmetry:** the region and the density are invariant under $y\mapsto -y$, so $$\bar y=0.$$ **$\bar x$.** With $dz\,dx$ inner, $$\iiint_E x\,dV=\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}\underbrace{\int_{0}^{1-z}x\,dx}_{=\frac{(1-z)^{2}}{2}}\,dz\,dy.$$ The $z$-integral: $$\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}\frac{(1-z)^{2}}{2}\,dz=\frac12\Big[-\frac{(1-z)^{3}}{3}\Big]_{0}^{1-y^{2}}=\frac{1-(y^{2})^{3}}{6}=\frac{1-y^{6}}{6}.$$ Then $$\iiint_E x\,dV=\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{1-y^{6}}{6}\,dy=\frac16\Big[y-\frac{y^{7}}{7}\Big]_{-1}^{1}=\frac16\cdot\frac{12}{7}=\frac{2}{7}, \qquad \bar x=\frac{2/7}{4/5}=\frac{5}{14}.$$ **$\bar z$.** $$\iiint_E z\,dV=\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{0}^{1-y^{2}}\underbrace{\int_{0}^{1-z}z\,dx}_{=z(1-z)}\,dz\,dy =\int_{-1}^{1}\Big[\frac{z^{2}}{2}-\frac{z^{3}}{3}\Big]_{0}^{1-y^{2}}dy.$$ So $$\iiint_E z\,dV=\frac12\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})^{2}\,dy-\frac13\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})^{3}\,dy.$$ Using $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^{2})^{3}\,dy=\tfrac{32}{35}$, $$\iiint_E z\,dV=\frac12\cdot\frac{16}{15}-\frac13\cdot\frac{32}{35}=\frac{8}{15}-\frac{32}{105}=\frac{56-32}{105}=\frac{24}{105}=\frac{8}{35}, \qquad \bar z=\frac{8/35}{4/5}=\frac{2}{7}.$$ --- ## Final answer $$\boxed{m=\frac{16}{5}},\qquad \boxed{(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)=\left(\frac{5}{14},\,0,\,\frac{2}{7}\right)}.$$ **Technique to remember.** For a solid bounded by a cylinder $z=g(y)$ and a slanted plane $x+z=1$ above the plane $z=0$, the natural iteration is $dx\,dz\,dy$: the innermost $x$-limit comes from the slanted plane, the middle $z$-limit from the cylinder, and the outer $y$-limits from where the cylinder meets the floor. A constant density turns mass into (density)$\times$(volume) and the center of mass into the plain centroid; any symmetry of the region collapses one coordinate immediately.
End of answer
