## Geometric set-up The surface $y=4-x^{2}-4z^{2}$ is an elliptic paraboloid opening in the negative $y$-direction. It meets the plane $y=0$ in the ellipse $$ x^{2}+4z^{2}=4 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \frac{x^{2}}{4}+z^{2}=1 . $$ Therefore the solid is $$ E=\{(x,y,z): x^{2}+4z^{2}\le 4,\quad 0\le y\le 4-x^{2}-4z^{2}\}. $$ The six iterated integrals come from choosing the order of integration and then projecting $E$ onto the coordinate plane of the two outer variables. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)

--- ## Projections and inner limits For an order such as $dz\,dy\,dx$, the outer two variables are $x$ and $y$, so we use the shadow on the $xy$-plane. Solving $y=4-x^{2}-4z^{2}$ for $z$ gives the inner limits $$ z=-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y},\qquad z=+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}. $$ Similarly: | order | projection (shadow) | inner limits | |---|---|---| | $dz\,dy\,dx$ | $0\le y\le 4-x^{2},\ -2\le x\le 2$ | $-\frac12\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}\le z\le \frac12\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}$ | | $dz\,dx\,dy$ | $0\le y\le 4,\ -\sqrt{4-y}\le x\le \sqrt{4-y}$ | same | | $dy\,dz\,dx$ | $-2\le x\le 2,\ -\sqrt{1-x^{2}/4}\le z\le \sqrt{1-x^{2}/4}$ | $0\le y\le 4-x^{2}-4z^{2}$ | | $dy\,dx\,dz$ | $-1\le z\le 1,\ -2\sqrt{1-z^{2}}\le x\le 2\sqrt{1-z^{2}}$ | same | | $dx\,dy\,dz$ | $-1\le z\le 1,\ 0\le y\le 4-4z^{2}$ | $-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}\le x\le \sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}$ | | $dx\,dz\,dy$ | $0\le y\le 4,\ -\sqrt{1-y/4}\le z\le \sqrt{1-y/4}$ | same | The three shadows are shown below. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_1.tex)

--- ## The six iterated integrals All of the following are equal to $\displaystyle \iiint_{E} f(x,y,z)\,dV$. $$ \boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{0}^{\,4-x^{2}}\int_{-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}^{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx\\[2mm] &\int_{0}^{4}\int_{-\sqrt{4-y}}^{\sqrt{4-y}}\int_{-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}^{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dx\,dy\\[2mm] &\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{1-x^{2}/4}}^{\sqrt{1-x^{2}/4}}\int_{0}^{\,4-x^{2}-4z^{2}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dz\,dx\\[2mm] &\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{-2\sqrt{1-z^{2}}}^{2\sqrt{1-z^{2}}}\int_{0}^{\,4-x^{2}-4z^{2}} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dx\,dz\\[2mm] &\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{0}^{\,4-4z^{2}}\int_{-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}}^{\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz\\[2mm] &\int_{0}^{4}\int_{-\sqrt{1-y/4}}^{\sqrt{1-y/4}}\int_{-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}}^{\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy \end{aligned}} $$ **Check.** Taking $f=1$ in any of the six integrals gives the volume $$ \iiint_{E} dV = \int_{0}^{4} \frac{\pi}{2}(4-y)\,dy = 4\pi, $$ which agrees with the cross-sectional-area computation.
