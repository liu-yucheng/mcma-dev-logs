# Expressing $\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV$ in six ways **Problem.** $E$ is the solid bounded by the four planes $$x=2,\qquad y=2,\qquad z=0,\qquad x+y-2z=2.$$ We must write the triple integral as an iterated integral in all six orders of integration. ## Step 1 — Identify the solid $E$ Each vertex is the intersection of three of the four planes: | Vertex | Intersection of | |:--:|:--| | $A=(2,2,0)$ | $x=2,\; y=2,\; z=0$ | | $B=(2,2,1)$ | $x=2,\; y=2,\; x+y-2z=2$ | | $C=(2,0,0)$ | $x=2,\; z=0,\; x+y-2z=2$ | | $D=(0,2,0)$ | $y=2,\; z=0,\; x+y-2z=2$ | So $E$ is the **tetrahedron** $ABCD$. Its volume is $$V=\tfrac16\bigl|\det(\vec{AB},\vec{AC},\vec{AD})\bigr|=\tfrac16|{-4}|=\tfrac23,$$ a useful number for checking later. A point inside (e.g. the centroid $(\tfrac32,\tfrac32,\tfrac14)$) satisfies $x+y-2z=\tfrac52\ge 2$, so the region is $$E=\Bigl\{(x,y,z):\ z\ge 0,\ x\le 2,\ y\le 2,\ x+y-2z\ge 2\Bigr\},$$ which we may write as the "sweep over the base, up to the slanted roof" form $$E=\Bigl\{(x,y,z):\ 0\le x\le 2,\ 0\le y\le 2,\ 0\le z\le \tfrac{x+y-2}{2}\Bigr\},$$ where the last interval is non-empty only when $x+y\ge 2$. (The bounds $x\ge0,\ y\ge0$ are automatic: they follow from $x+y\ge2$ together with $x\le2,\ y\le2$.) --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)

--- ## Step 2 — The three coordinate projections The limits of an iterated integral are just the **shadow (projection)** of $E$ on the relevant coordinate plane together with the height over each point. * **$xy$-plane.** Setting the slanted roof equal to the floor $z=0$ gives $x+y=2$. The shadow is the triangle with vertices $(0,2),(2,2),(2,0)$, bounded by $x=2$, $y=2$ and $x+y=2$. * **$xz$-plane.** Projecting out $y$ (using $y=2$ on the slanted plane, i.e. $x-2z=0$) gives the triangle $(0,0),(2,0),(2,1)$, bounded by $z=0$, $x=2$ and $z=x/2$. * **$yz$-plane.** By the symmetry $x\leftrightarrow y$, the shadow is the triangle $(0,0),(2,0),(2,1)$ bounded by $z=0$, $y=2$ and $z=y/2$. For a point $(x,y)$ in the $xy$-shadow, the vertical segment of $E$ runs from the floor $z=0$ up to the slanted roof $z=\dfrac{x+y-2}{2}$. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_1.tex)

--- ## Step 3 — Horizontal cross-sections (for orders ending in $dz$) When $z$ is the **outer** variable, first find the range $0\le z\le 1$ (the top vertex $B$ has $z=1$). At a fixed height $z$, the constraint $x+y-2z\ge 2$ becomes $x+y\ge 2+2z$, so the cross-section is the nested triangle with vertices $$(2z,2),\quad (2,2),\quad (2,2z).$$ As $z$ grows from $0$ to $1$ these triangles shrink from the base to the single point $(2,2)$. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_3.tex)

--- ## Step 4 — The six iterated integrals **(1) $dz\,dy\,dx$ — sweep the $xy$-shadow, roof last.** Outer $x\in[0,2]$; for fixed $x$ the shadow needs $x+y\ge2$ and $y\le2$, so $y\in[2-x,\,2]$; then $z\in\bigl[0,\tfrac{x+y-2}{2}\bigr]$: $$\int_0^2\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\!\int_0^{(x+y-2)/2} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx.$$ **(2) $dz\,dx\,dy$ — the mirror image of (1).** Here $y\in[0,2]$ is outer and $x\in[2-y,\,2]$: $$\int_0^2\!\int_{2-y}^{2}\!\int_0^{(x+y-2)/2} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dx\,dy.$$ **(3) $dy\,dz\,dx$ — project on the $xz$-plane, solve the roof for $y$.** For fixed $x$, the shadow in $(z,y)$ has $z\in[0,\,x/2]$ and the slanted plane gives $y\ge 2+2z-x$, while $y\le2$: $$\int_0^2\!\int_0^{x/2}\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dz\,dx.$$ **(4) $dy\,dx\,dz$ — slice horizontally, $z$ outermost.** For fixed $z\in[0,1]$ the cross-section is the triangle $(2z,2),(2,2),(2,2z)$, so $x\in[2z,\,2]$ and $y\in[2+2z-x,\,2]$: $$\int_0^1\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dy\,dx\,dz.$$ **(5) $dx\,dz\,dy$ — the $x\leftrightarrow y$ mirror of (3).** $$\int_0^2\!\int_0^{y/2}\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dz\,dy.$$ **(6) $dx\,dy\,dz$ — the $x\leftrightarrow y$ mirror of (4).** $$\int_0^1\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz.$$ ## Verification Setting $f\equiv1$, every one of the six integrals evaluates to $\tfrac23$, exactly the tetrahedron volume $\tfrac16|{-4}|=\tfrac23$ found in Step 1 — so all six setups describe the same solid. $$\boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\text{(1)}\ \int_0^2\!\int_{2-x}^{2}\!\int_0^{(x+y-2)/2} f\,dz\,dy\,dx &&\qquad\text{(2)}\ \int_0^2\!\int_{2-y}^{2}\!\int_0^{(x+y-2)/2} f\,dz\,dx\,dy\\[4pt] &\text{(3)}\ \int_0^2\!\int_0^{x/2}\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f\,dy\,dz\,dx &&\qquad\text{(4)}\ \int_0^1\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\int_{2+2z-x}^{2} f\,dy\,dx\,dz\\[4pt] &\text{(5)}\ \int_0^2\!\int_0^{y/2}\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f\,dx\,dz\,dy &&\qquad\text{(6)}\ \int_0^1\!\int_{2z}^{2}\!\int_{2+2z-y}^{2} f\,dx\,dy\,dz \end{aligned}}$$
