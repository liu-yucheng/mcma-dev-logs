Answer
The given iterated integral is $$ \int_{0}^{2}\int_{0}^{2-y}\int_{0}^{4-y^{2}}dx\,dz\,dy. $$ The order of integration is $dx\,dz\,dy$: innermost $x$, middle $z$, outermost $y$. Reading the limits: $$ 0\le y\le 2,\qquad 0\le z\le 2-y,\qquad 0\le x\le 4-y^{2}. $$ Hence the solid is $$ S=\{(x,y,z):0\le y\le 2,\ 0\le z\le 2-y,\ 0\le x\le4-y^{2}\}. $$ It lies in the first octant. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)

--- For a fixed $y$, the inequalities $$ 0\le z\le 2-y,\qquad 0\le x\le 4-y^{2} $$ describe a rectangle in the $xz$-plane. Thus $S$ is a stack of rectangles parallel to the $xz$-plane: at height $y$ the rectangle is $$ [0,4-y^{2}]\times[0,2-y] $$ in $(x,z)$-coordinates. The projection onto the $yz$-plane is the triangle $$ D_{yz}=\{(y,z):0\le y\le2,\ 0\le z\le2-y\}, $$ with vertices $(0,0),(2,0),(0,2)$. --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_1.tex)

--- The boundary surfaces are: - $z=0$: bottom (the $xy$-plane over $D_{xy}$); - $z=2-y$: top slanted plane; - $x=0$: left vertical wall; - $y=0$: back vertical wall (rectangle $0\le x\le4$, $0\le z\le2$); - $x=4-y^{2}$: right parabolic cylinder; - $y=2$: the degenerate point $(0,2,0)$. The volume, included as a check, is $$ V=\int_{0}^{2}(4-y^{2})(2-y)\,dy =\int_{0}^{2}(y^{3}-2y^{2}-4y+8)\,dy =\left[\frac{y^{4}}{4}-\frac{2y^{3}}{3}-2y^{2}+8y\right]_{0}^{2} =\frac{20}{3}. $$ Therefore, the solid is the curved wedge $$ \boxed{S=\{(x,y,z):0\le y\le2,\ 0\le z\le2-y,\ 0\le x\le4-y^{2}\}}. $$
