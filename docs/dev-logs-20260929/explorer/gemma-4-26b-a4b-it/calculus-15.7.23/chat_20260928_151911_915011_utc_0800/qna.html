In the first octant, all coordinates are non-negative: $$x \ge 0, \quad y \ge 0, \quad z \ge 0$$ The region is bounded by: - The cylinder: $y^2 + z^2 = 1 \implies z = \sqrt{1 - y^2}$ (since $z \ge 0$). - The plane: $y = x$. - The plane: $x = 1$. - The coordinate planes: $x=0, y=0, z=0$. ### 2. Set up the Triple Integral We can set up the integral using the order $dz \, dy \, dx$. - For a fixed $x$ and $y$, $z$ ranges from the $xy$-plane ($z=0$) to the cylinder ($z = \sqrt{1 - y^2}$). - For a fixed $x$, $y$ ranges from the $x$-axis ($y=0$) to the line $y = x$. - $x$ ranges from $0$ to $1$. Thus, the volume $V$ is: $$ V = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{x} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} dz \, dy \, dx $$ Alternatively, if we integrate in the order $dz \, dx \, dy$: - For a fixed $y$, $x$ ranges from the line $x = y$ to the line $x = 1$. - $y$ ranges from $0$ to $1$. $$ V = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{y}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} dz \, dx \, dy $$ --- ## Part (b): Evaluating the Integral We will evaluate the second form, $V = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{y}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} dz \, dx \, dy$, as it is slightly simpler. ### Step 1: Integrate with respect to $z$ $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} dz = [z]_0^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} = \sqrt{1 - y^2} $$ ### Step 2: Integrate with respect to $x$ $$ \int_{y}^{1} \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dx = \sqrt{1 - y^2} \cdot [x]_y^1 = (1 - y) \sqrt{1 - y^2} $$ ### Step 3: Integrate with respect to $y$ We need to calculate: $$ V = \int_{0}^{1} (1 - y) \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy $$ We can split this into two separate integrals: $$ V = \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy - \int_{0}^{1} y \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy $$ **Integral 1:** $\int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy$ This represents the area of a quarter-circle with radius $1$. $$ \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy = \frac{1}{4} \pi (1)^2 = \frac{\pi}{4} $$ **Integral 2:** $\int_{0}^{1} y \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy$ Use substitution: $u = 1 - y^2$, then $du = -2y \, dy \implies y \, dy = -\frac{1}{2} du$.
