The problem asks us to express the triple integral $\iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV$ as an iterated integral in six different ways, where $E$ is the solid bounded by the surfaces $y = 4 - x^2 - 4z^2$ and $y = 0$. ### Step 1: Analyze the Region $E$ The region $E$ is bounded by: 1. The elliptic paraboloid $y = 4 - x^2 - 4z^2$ (which opens towards the negative $y$-direction). 2. The plane $y = 0$. The intersection of these two surfaces is found by setting $y = 0$ in the equation of the paraboloid: $$0 = 4 - x^2 - 4z^2 \implies x^2 + 4z^2 = 4 \implies \frac{x^2}{4} + z^2 = 1$$ This intersection is an ellipse in the $xz$-plane (at $y=0$). The solid $E$ consists of all points $(x, y, z)$ such that $0 \le y \le 4 - x^2 - 4z^2$. For $y$ to be non-negative, we must have $x^2 + 4z^2 \le 4$, which is the interior of the ellipse in the $xz$-plane. ### Step 2: Determine the Six Iterated Integrals The six possible orders of integration for a triple integral $\iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV$ are: $dy \, dz \, dx, \quad dy \, dx \, dz, \quad dz \, dy \, dx, \quad dz \, dx \, dy, \quad dx \, dy \, dz, \quad dx \, dz \, dy$. #### 1. Order $dy \, dz \, dx$ The projection of $E$ onto the $xz$-plane is the ellipse $x^2 + 4z^2 \le 4$. - $x$ ranges from $-2$ to $2$. - For a fixed $x$, $4z^2 \le 4 - x^2 \implies z^2 \le 1 - (x/2)^2 \implies z \in \left[ -\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^2}, \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^2} \right]$. - For fixed $x$ and $z$, $y$ ranges from $0$ to $4 - x^2 - 4z^2$. $$\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^2}}^{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-x^2}} \int_{0}^{4-x^2-4z^2} f(x, y, z) \, dy \, dz \, dx$$ #### 2. Order $dy \, dx \, dz$ The projection onto the $xz$-plane is the same ellipse. - $z$ ranges from $-1$ to $1$. - For a fixed $z$, $x^2 \le 4 - 4z^2 \implies x \in \left[ -2\sqrt{1-z^2}, 2\sqrt{1-z^2} \right]$. - For fixed $z$ and $x$, $y$ ranges from $0$ to $4 - x^2 - 4z^2$. $$\int_{-1}^{1} \int_{-2\sqrt{1-z^2}}^{2\sqrt{1-z^2}} \int_{0}^{4-x^2-4z^2} f(x, y, z) \, dy \, dx \, dz$$ #### 3. Order $dx \, dz \, dy$ The projection onto the $yz$-plane is the region bounded by $y = 4 - 4z^2$ and $y = 0$. - $y$ ranges from $0$ to $4$. - For a fixed $y$, $4z^2 \le 4 - y \implies z^2 \le 1 - y/4 \implies z \in \left[ -\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-y}, \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-y} \right]$. - For fixed $y$ and $z$, $x^2 = 4 - y - 4z^2 \implies x \in \left[ -\sqrt{4-y-4z^2}, \sqrt{4-y-4z^2} \right]$. $$\int_{0}^{4} \int_{-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-y}}^{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4-y}} \int_{-\sqrt{4-y-4z^2}}^{\sqrt{4-y-4z^2}} f(x, y, z) \, dx \, dz \, dy$$ #### 6.
