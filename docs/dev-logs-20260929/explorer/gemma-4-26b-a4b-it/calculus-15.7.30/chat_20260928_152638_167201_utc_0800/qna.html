# Solution: Expressing the Triple Integral in Six Different Ways The problem asks us to express the triple integral $\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV$ in six different orders of integration, where the solid $E$ is bounded by the surfaces: 1. $y^2 + z^2 = 9$ (a cylinder of radius 3 centered on the $x$-axis) 2. $x = -2$ (a plane perpendicular to the $x$-axis) 3. $x = 2$ (a plane perpendicular to the $x$-axis) ## 1. Analysis of the Solid $E$ The solid $E$ is a cylinder of radius $3$ whose central axis is the $x$-axis, extending from $x = -2$ to $x = 2$. ### Projections onto Coordinate Planes: - **$yz$-plane**: The projection of $E$ onto the $yz$-plane is the disk $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$. - **$xy$-plane**: The projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane is the rectangle defined by $-2 \le x \le 2$ and $-3 \le y \le 3$. - **$xz$-plane**: The projection of $E$ onto the $xz$-plane is the rectangle defined by $-2 \le x \le 2$ and $-3 \le z \le 3$. ## 2. Deriving the Six Iterated Integrals There are $3! = 6$ possible orders of integration: $dz\,dy\,dx$, $dz\,dx\,dy$, $dy\,dz\,dx$, $dy\,dx\,dz$, $dx\,dy\,dz$, and $dx\,dz\,dy$. ### Case 1: $dz\,dy\,dx$ We integrate with respect to $z$ first. For a fixed $(x, y)$, $z$ ranges from the bottom of the cylinder to the top: $-\sqrt{9 - y^2} \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}$. Then we integrate over the projection in the $xy$-plane (the rectangle). The bounds are: - $-2 \le x \le 2$ - $-3 \le y \le 3$ - $-\sqrt{9 - y^2} \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}$ $$\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dz \, dy \, dx$$ ### Case 2: $dz\,dx\,dy$ Similar to Case 1, we integrate $z$ first. The order of $x$ and $y$ is swapped. The bounds are: - $-3 \le y \le 3$ - $-2 \le x \le 2$ - $-\sqrt{9 - y^2} \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}$ $$\int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dz \, dx \, dy$$ ### Case 3: $dy\,dz\,dx$ We integrate with respect to $y$ first. For a fixed $(x, z)$, $y$ ranges from $-\sqrt{9 - z^2}$ to $\sqrt{9 - z^2}$. The projection in the $xz$-plane is a rectangle. The bounds are: - $-2 \le x \le 2$ - $-3 \le z \le 3$ - $-\sqrt{9 - z^2} \le y \le \sqrt{9 - z^2}$ $$\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dy \, dz \, dx$$ ### Case 4: $dy\,dx\,dz$ Similar to Case 3, we swap $x$ and $z$. The bounds are: - $-3 \le z \le 3$ - $-2 \le x \le 2$ - $-\sqrt{9 - z^2} \le y \le \sqrt{9 - z^2}$ $$\int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dy \, dx \, dz$$ ### Case 5: $dx\,dy\,dz$ We integrate with respect to $x$ first. For a fixed $(y, z)$, $x$ ranges from $-2$ to $2$. The projection in the $yz$-plane is a disk. The bounds are: - $-3 \le z \le 3$ - $-\sqrt{9 - z^2} \le y \le \sqrt{9 - z^2}$ - $-2 \le x \le 2$ $$\int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} \int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z) \, dx \, dy \, dz$$ ### Case 6: $dx\,dz\,dy$ Similar to Case 5, we swap $y$ and $z$. The bounds are: - $-3 \le y \le 3$ - $-\sqrt{9 - y^2} \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}$ - $-2 \le x \le 2$ $$\int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z) \, dx \, dz \, dy$$ ## Final Answer Summary The six ways to express the integral $\iiint_E f(x,y,z) \, dV$ are: 1. $\displaystyle \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dz \, dy \, dx$ 2. $\displaystyle \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dz \, dx \, dy$ 3. $\displaystyle \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dy \, dz \, dx$ 4. $\displaystyle \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} f(x,y,z) \, dy \, dx \, dz$ 5. $\displaystyle \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} \int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z) \, dx \, dy \, dz$ 6. $\displaystyle \int_{-3}^{3} \int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{-2}^{2} f(x,y,z) \, dx \, dz \, dy$ $\boxed{}$
