# Problem Statement Find the average value of the function $f(x, y, z) = x^2z + y^2z$ over the region $E$ enclosed by the paraboloid $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$ and the plane $z = 0$. The average value of a function $f(x, y, z)$ over a solid region $E$ is defined as: $$ f_{\text{ave}} = \frac{1}{V(E)} \iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV $$ where $V(E)$ is the volume of the region $E$. ## Step 1: Describe the region $E$ The region $E$ is bounded by: 1. The paraboloid $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$. 2. The plane $z = 0$. To find the projection of the region onto the $xy$-plane, we look at the intersection where $z = 0$: $$ 0 = 1 - x^2 - y^2 \implies x^2 + y^2 = 1 $$ This is a unit circle in the $xy$-plane. Since the region has circular symmetry, we use **cylindrical coordinates**: $x = r \cos \theta$, $y = r \sin \theta$, $z = z$. The differential volume element is $dV = r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta$. The bounds for the region $E$ in cylindrical coordinates are: - $0 \le \theta \le 2\pi$ - $0 \le r \le 1$ - $0 \le z \le 1 - r^2$ ## Step 2: Calculate the volume $V(E)$ The volume is given by: $$ V(E) = \iiint_E 1 \, dV = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-r^2} r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta $$ First, integrate with respect to $z$: $$ \int_0^{1-r^2} r \, dz = r [z]_0^{1-r^2} = r(1 - r^2) = r - r^3 $$ Next, integrate with respect to $r$: $$ \int_0^1 (r - r^3) \, dr = \left[ \frac{1}{2}r^2 - \frac{1}{4}r^4 \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{4} $$ Finally, integrate with respect to $\theta$: $$ V(E) = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{1}{4} \, d\theta = \frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2} $$ ## Step 3: Calculate the integral of $f(x, y, z)$ over $E$ The function is $f(x, y, z) = x^2z + y^2z = (x^2 + y^2)z$. In cylindrical coordinates, $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$, so: $$ f(r, \theta, z) = r^2 z $$ The integral is: $$ I = \iiint_E (r^2 z) \, dV = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-r^2} (r^2 z) \cdot r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-r^2} r^3 z \, dz \, dr \, d\theta $$ First, integrate with respect to $z$: $$ \int_0^{1-r^2} r^3 z \, dz = r^3 \left[ \frac{1}{2}z^2 \right]_0^{1-r^2} = \frac{1}{2} r^3 (1 - r^2)^2 $$ $$ = \frac{1}{2} r^3 (1 - 2r^2 + r^4) = \frac{1}{2} (r^3 - 2r^5 + r^7) $$ Next, integrate with respect to $r$: $$ \int_0^1 \frac{1}{2} (r^3 - 2r^5 + r^7) \, dr = \frac{1}{2} \left[ \frac{1}{4}r^4 - \frac{2}{6}r^6 + \frac{1}{8}r^8 \right]_0^1 $$ $$ = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{8} \right) $$ Common denominator is 24: $$ \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{6}{24} - \frac{8}{24} + \frac{3}{24} = \frac{1}{24} $$ So the integral is: $$ \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{24} = \frac{1}{48} $$ Finally, integrate with respect to $\theta$: $$ I = \int_0^{2\pi} \frac{1}{48} \, d\theta = \frac{2\pi}{48} = \frac{\pi}{24} $$ ## Step 4: Calculate the average value The average value is: $$ f_{\text{ave}} = \frac{I}{V(E)} = \frac{\pi/24}{\pi/2} = \frac{\pi}{24} \cdot \frac{2}{\pi} = \frac{2}{24} = \frac{1}{12} $$ $$\boxed{\frac{1}{12}}$$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
