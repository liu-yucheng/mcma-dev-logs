# Problem Solution The goal is to find the mass, center of mass, and moment of inertia about the $z$-axis for a solid $E$ bounded by the cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$, the planes $x = 0$, $y = 3x$, and $z = 0$ in the first octant, with density function $\rho(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2$. ## 1. Setup and Limits of Integration First, let's define the region $E$. The solid is in the first octant, so $x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$, and $z \ge 0$. The boundaries are: - Cylinder: $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$ - Plane 1: $x = 0$ - Plane 2: $y = 3x \implies x = y/3$ - Plane 3: $z = 0$ From $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$ and $z \ge 0$, we have $0 \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}$. For a fixed $y$ and $z$, the plane $x = 0$ and $x = y/3$ give $0 \le x \le y/3$. The cylinder constraint $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$ implies $y$ can range from $0$ to $3$. Thus, the limits for the triple integral are: $$ 0 \le y \le 3, \quad 0 \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^2}, \quad 0 \le x \le \frac{y}{3} $$ ## 2. Part (a): The Mass The mass $M$ is given by: $$ M = \iiint_E \rho(x, y, z) \, dV = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{0}^{y/3} (x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ First, compute the inner integral with respect to $x$: $$ \int_{0}^{y/3} (x^2 + y^2) \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} + xy^2 \right]_{0}^{y/3} = \frac{(y/3)^3}{3} + \frac{y}{3}y^2 = \frac{y^3}{81} + \frac{y^3}{3} = \frac{1+27}{81} y^3 = \frac{28}{81}y^3 $$ Next, integrate with respect to $z$: $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \frac{28}{81} y^3 \, dz = \frac{28}{81} y^3 \sqrt{9 - y^2} $$ Finally, integrate with respect to $y$: $$ M = \int_{0}^{3} \frac{28}{81} y^3 \sqrt{9 - y^2} \, dy $$ Let $u = 9 - y^2$, then $du = -2y \, dy \implies y \, dy = -\frac{1}{2} du$. Also $y^2 = 9 - u$. When $y=0, u=9$; when $y=3, u=0$. $$ M = \frac{28}{81} \int_{9}^{0} (9 - u) \sqrt{u} \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) du = \frac{14}{81} \int_{0}^{9} (9u^{1/2} - u^{3/2}) \, du $$ $$ M = \frac{14}{81} \left[ 9 \cdot \frac{2}{3} u^{3/2} - \frac{2}{5} u^{5/2} \right]_{0}^{9} = \frac{14}{81} \left[ 6(27) - \frac{2}{5}(243) \right] = \frac{14}{81} \left[ 162 - \frac{486}{5} \right] $$ $$ M = \frac{14}{81} \left[ \frac{810 - 486}{5} \right] = \frac{14}{81} \cdot \frac{324}{5} = 14 \cdot \frac{4}{5} = \frac{56}{5} = 11.2 $$ The mass is $\boxed{11.200}$. ## 3. Part (b): The Center of Mass The center of mass $(\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z})$ is calculated using: $$ \bar{x} = \frac{M_x}{M}, \quad \bar{y} = \frac{M_y}{M}, \quad \bar{z} = \frac{M_z}{M} $$ where $M_x = \iiint_E x \rho \, dV$, $M_y = \iiint_E y \rho \, dV$, and $M_z = \iiint_E z \rho \, dV$. ### Calculating $M_x$: $$ M_x = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{0}^{y/3} x(x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ Inner integral: $$ \int_{0}^{y/3} (x^3 + xy^2) \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^4}{4} + \frac{x^2y^2}{2} \right]_{0}^{y/3} = \frac{y^4}{4 \cdot 81} + \frac{y^2 \cdot y^2}{2 \cdot 9} = \frac{y^4}{324} + \frac{y^4}{18} = \frac{1 + 18}{324} y^4 = \frac{19}{324} y^4 $$ Middle integral: $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \frac{19}{324} y^4 \, dz = \frac{19}{324} y^4 \sqrt{9 - y^2} $$ Outer integral: $$ M_x = \int_{0}^{3} \frac{19}{324} y^4 \sqrt{9 - y^2} \, dy $$ Using $y = 3 \sin \theta, dy = 3 \cos \theta \, d\theta$: $$ M_x = \frac{19}{324} \int_{0}^{\pi/2} (3 \sin \theta)^4 (3 \cos \theta) (3 \cos \theta) \, d\theta = \frac{19}{324} \cdot 729 \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^4 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta $$ $$ M_x = \frac{19 \cdot 9}{4} \cdot \frac{\pi}{32} = \frac{171 \pi}{128} $$ So $\bar{x} = \frac{171 \pi / 128}{56/5} = \frac{171 \pi}{128} \cdot \frac{5}{56} = \frac{855 \pi}{7168} \approx 0.374729...$ ### Calculating $M_y$: $$ M_y = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{0}^{y/3} y(x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ Inner integral: $\frac{28}{81} y^4$. Middle integral: $\frac{28}{81} y^4 \sqrt{9 - y^2}$. Outer integral: $$ M_y = \int_{0}^{3} \frac{28}{81} y^4 \sqrt{9 - y^2} \, dy = \frac{28}{81} \cdot 729 \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^4 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta = 28 \cdot 9 \cdot \frac{\pi}{32} = \frac{63 \pi}{8} $$ So $\bar{y} = \frac{63 \pi / 8}{56/5} = \frac{63 \pi}{8} \cdot \frac{5}{56} = \frac{9 \pi}{8} \cdot \frac{5}{8} = \frac{45 \pi}{64} \approx 2.20893...$ ### Calculating $M_z$: $$ M_z = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_{0}^{y/3} z(x^2 + y^2) \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ Inner integral: $\frac{28}{81} y^3$. Middle integral: $\int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \frac{28}{81} y^3 z \, dz = \frac{28}{81} y^3 \left[ \frac{z^2}{2} \right]_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} = \frac{14}{81} y^3 (9 - y^2) = \frac{14}{81} (9y^3 - y^5)$ Outer integral: $$ M_z = \frac{14}{81} \int_{0}^{3} (9y^3 - y^5) \, dy = \frac{14}{81} \left[ \frac{9y^4}{4} - \frac{y^6}{6} \right]_{0}^{3} = \frac{14}{81} \left[ \frac{729}{4} - \frac{729}{6} \right] = \frac{14 \cdot 729}{81} \left[ \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} \right] = 126 \cdot \frac{1}{12} = 10.5 $$ So $\bar{z} = \frac{10.5}{11.2} = \frac{105}{112} = \frac{15}{16} = 0.9375$. The center of mass is $(\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}) = \boxed{(0.375, 2.209, 0.938)}$. ## 4. Part (c): The Moment of Inertia about the $z$-axis The moment of inertia about the $z$-axis is: $$ I_z = \iiint_E (x^2 + y^2) \rho(x, y, z) \, dV = \iiint_E (x^2 + y^2)^2 \, dV $$ Inner integral (with respect to $x$): $$ \int_{0}^{y/3} (x^2 + y^2)^2 \, dx = \int_{0}^{y/3} (x^4 + 2x^2y^2 + y^4) \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^5}{5} + \frac{2x^3y^2}{3} + xy^4 \right]_{0}^{y/3} $$ $$ = \frac{y^5}{5 \cdot 243} + \frac{2y^5}{3 \cdot 27} + \frac{y^5}{3} = \frac{y^5}{1215} + \frac{2y^5}{81} + \frac{y^5}{3} = \frac{1 + 30 + 405}{1215} y^5 = \frac{436}{1215} y^5 $$ Middle integral: $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \frac{436}{1215} y^5 \, dz = \frac{436}{1215} y^5 \sqrt{9 - y^2} $$ Outer integral: $$ I_z = \int_{0}^{3} \frac{436}{1215} y^5 \sqrt{9 - y^2} \, dy $$ Using $y = 3 \sin \theta, dy = 3 \cos \theta \, d\theta$: $$ I_z = \frac{436}{1215} \int_{0}^{\pi/2} (3 \sin \theta)^5 (3 \cos \theta) (3 \cos \theta) \, d\theta = \frac{436}{1215} \cdot 3^7 \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^5 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta $$ $$ I_z = \frac{436 \cdot 2187}{1215} \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^5 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta = \frac{436 \cdot 9}{5} \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^5 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta $$ Using the formula $\int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^m \theta \cos^n \theta \, d\theta = \frac{\Gamma(\frac{m+1}{2})\Gamma(\frac{n+1}{2})}{2\Gamma(\frac{m+n+2}{2})}$: For $m=5, n=2$: $$ \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \sin^5 \theta \cos^2 \theta \, d\theta = \frac{\Gamma(3)\Gamma(1.5)}{2\Gamma(4.5)} = \frac{2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}}{2 \cdot \frac{105}{16}\sqrt{\pi}} = \frac{1}{105/8} = \frac{8}{105} $$ Thus, $$ I_z = \frac{436 \cdot 9}{5} \cdot \frac{8}{105} = \frac{436 \cdot 3 \cdot 8}{5 \cdot 35} = \frac{10464}{175} \approx 59.79428... $$ The moment of inertia is $\boxed{59.794}$. --- No graphical results.
