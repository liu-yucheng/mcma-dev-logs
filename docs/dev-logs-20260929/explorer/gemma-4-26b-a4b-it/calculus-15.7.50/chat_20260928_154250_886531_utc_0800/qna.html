Problem Identification and Interpretation The problem states that the solid $E$ is bounded by: - The cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ (noting the typo in the image as $y^2 + x^2 = 9$). - The plane $x = 0$. - The plane $y = 3x$. - The plane $z = 0$. In the first octant ($x, y, z \ge 0$), these surfaces define a wedge-shaped region in the $xy$-plane. However, the solid is not bounded in the $z$-direction. Based on standard calculus textbook problems (specifically Stewart's *Calculus: Early Transcendentals*), there is an implicit or omitted boundary plane $z = 3$. We will solve the problem assuming this boundary is $z = 3$. **Given:** - Boundary 1: $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ (radius $R=3$) - Boundary 2: $x = 0$ (which is the $yz$-plane, i.e., $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$) - Boundary 3: $y = 3x$ (which means $\tan \theta = 3$, so $\theta = \arctan(3)$) - Boundary 4: $z = 0$ - Boundary 5 (Assumed): $z = 3$ - Density function: $\rho(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2$ ### 2. Setup in Cylindrical Coordinates We use cylindrical coordinates $(r, \theta, z)$: - $x = r \cos \theta$ - $y = r \sin \theta$ - $z = z$ - $dV = r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta$ - $\rho(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2 = r^2$ The bounds for the triple integral are: - $0 \le z \le 3$ - $0 \le r \le 3$ - $\arctan(3) \le \theta \le \frac{\pi}{2}$ ### 3. Calculating the Quantities #### (a) The Mass $m$ The mass is given by the triple integral of the density over the solid $E$: $$ m = \iiint_E \rho(x, y, z) \, dV = \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} \int_0^3 \int_0^3 r^2 \cdot r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta $$ $$ m = \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} d\theta \int_0^3 r^3 \, dr \int_0^3 dz $$ $$ \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} d\theta = \frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan(3) $$ $$ \int_0^3 r^3 \, dr = \left[ \frac{r^4}{4} \right]_0^3 = \frac{81}{4} $$ $$ \int_0^3 dz = 3 $$ Multiplying these: $$ m = \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan(3) \right) \cdot \frac{81}{4} \cdot 3 = \frac{243}{4} \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan(3) \right) $$ Using the values: $\frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan(3) \approx 1.5708 - 1.2490 = 0.32175$ $$ m \approx 60.75 \times 0.32175 \approx 19.546 $$ #### (b) The Center of Mass $(\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z})$ The coordinates are $\bar{x} = \frac{M_{yz}}{m}$, $\bar{y} = \frac{M_{xz}}{m}$, and $\bar{z} = \frac{M_{xy}}{m}$. **1. $M_{yz}$ (Moment with respect to $yz$-plane):** $$ M_{yz} = \iiint_E x \rho \, dV = \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} \int_0^3 \int_0^3 (r \cos \theta) r^3 \, dz \, dr \, d\theta $$ $$ M_{yz} = \left( \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} \cos \theta \, d\theta \right) \left( \int_0^3 r^4 \, dr \right) (3) $$ $$ \int_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} \cos \theta \, d\theta = [\sin \theta]_{\arctan(3)}^{\pi/2} = 1 -
