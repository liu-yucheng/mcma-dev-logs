# Problem Statement Evaluate the triple integral: $$ \iiint_E z \, dV $$ where $E$ is the region in the first octant bounded by the cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$ and the planes $x = 0$, $y = 3x$, and $z = 0$. # Solution ### 1. Understanding the Region $E$ First, let's identify the boundaries of the region $E$ in the first octant ($x \ge 0, y \ge 0, z \ge 0$): - **Cylinder**: $y^2 + z^2 = 9$. In the first octant, this represents a quarter-cylinder along the $x$-axis with radius $3$. - **Plane 1**: $x = 0$. This is the $yz$-plane. - **Plane 2**: $y = 3x \implies x = \frac{y}{3}$. This is a vertical plane passing through the origin. - **Plane 3**: $z = 0$. This is the $xy$-plane. The region $E$ is the volume where $x$ is bounded between the planes $x = 0$ and $x = \frac{y}{3}$, and the $(y,z)$ cross-section is bounded by the quarter-disk $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$ in the first quadrant of the $yz$-plane. ### 2. Setting up the Triple Integral We can set up the integral by integrating with respect to $x$ first, then $z$, and finally $y$. The limits of integration are: - For a fixed $y$ and $z$, $x$ ranges from $0$ to $\frac{y}{3}$. - The projection of the region onto the $yz$-plane is the quarter-disk defined by $y^2 + z^2 \le 9$ with $y \ge 0$ and $z \ge 0$. - Thus, for a fixed $y$, $z$ ranges from $0$ to $\sqrt{9 - y^2}$. - Finally, $y$ ranges from $0$ to $3$. The triple integral becomes: $$ I = \int_0^3 \int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \int_0^{y/3} z \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ ### 3. Evaluating the Integral **Step 1: Integrate with respect to $x$** $$ \int_0^{y/3} z \, dx = z \Big[ x \Big]_0^{y/3} = z \left( \frac{y}{3} - 0 \right) = \frac{yz}{3} $$ **Step 2: Integrate with respect to $z$** $$ \int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} \frac{yz}{3} \, dz = \frac{y}{3} \int_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} z \, dz = \frac{y}{3} \left[ \frac{z^2}{2} \right]_0^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} $$ $$ = \frac{y}{3} \left( \frac{(\sqrt{9-y^2})^2}{2} - 0 \right) = \frac{y}{3} \cdot \frac{9 - y^2}{2} = \frac{9y - y^3}{6} $$ **Step 3: Integrate with respect to $y$** $$ I = \int_0^3 \frac{9y - y^3}{6} \, dy = \frac{1}{6} \int_0^3 (9y - y^3) \, dy $$ $$ = \frac{1}{6} \left[ \frac{9y^2}{2} - \frac{y^4}{4} \right]_0^3 $$ $$ = \frac{1}{6} \left( \left( \frac{9(3)^2}{2} - \frac{3^4}{4} \right) - 0 \right) $$ $$ = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{81}{2} - \frac{81}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{162 - 81}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{81}{4} \right) $$ $$ = \frac{81}{24} = \frac{27}{8} $$ ### Final Answer The value of the triple integral is: $$ \boxed{\frac{27}{8}} $$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
