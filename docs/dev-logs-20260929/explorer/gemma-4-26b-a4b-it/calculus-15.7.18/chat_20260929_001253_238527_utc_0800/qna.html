To evaluate the triple integral $\iiint_E z \, dV$, we first need to understand the geometry of the region $E$. ### 1. Description of the Region $E$ The region $E$ is bounded by the following surfaces in the first octant ($x \ge 0, y \ge 0, z \ge 0$): - The cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$. In the first octant, this is $z = \sqrt{9 - y^2}$. - The plane $x = 0$. - The plane $y = 3x$, which can be rewritten as $x = \frac{y}{3}$. - The plane $z = 0$. To find the projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane, we look at the bounds on $x$ and $y$. - The cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$ intersects the $xy$-plane ($z=0$) at $y^2 = 9$, which means $y = 3$ in the first octant. - The bounds for $y$ are therefore $0 \le y \le 3$. - For a fixed $y$, the region in the $xy$-plane is bounded by $x = 0$ and $x = \frac{y}{3}$. Thus, the projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane is a triangle with vertices at $(0,0)$, $(0,3)$, and $(1,3)$. ### 2. Setting up the Triple Integral We can set up the triple integral using the order $dz \, dx \, dy$ or $dz \, dy \, dx$. Let's use the order $dz \, dx \, dy$. For a fixed point $(x, y)$ in the projection: - $z$ varies from $0$ to $\sqrt{9 - y^2}$. - For a fixed $y$, $x$ varies from $0$ to $\frac{y}{3}$. - $y$ varies from $0$ to $3$. The integral is: $$ \iiint_E z \, dV = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{y/3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} z \, dz \, dx \, dy $$ ### 3. Evaluation of the Integral **Step 1: Integrate with respect to $z$** $$ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} z \, dz = \left[ \frac{z^2}{2} \right]_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} = \frac{1}{2} \left( (\sqrt{9-y^2})^2 - 0^2 \right) = \frac{9 - y^2}{2} $$ **Step 2: Integrate with respect to $x$** $$ \int_{0}^{y/3} \frac{9 - y^2}{2} \, dx = \frac{9 - y^2}{2} \int_{0}^{y/3} dx = \frac{9 - y^2}{2} \left[ x \right]_{0}^{y/3} = \frac{9 - y^2}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{3} = \frac{y(9 - y^2)}{6} = \frac{9y - y^3}{6} $$ **Step 3: Integrate with respect to $y$** $$ \int_{0}^{3} \frac{9y - y^3}{6} \, dy = \frac{1}{6} \int_{0}^{3} (9y - y^3) \, dy = \frac{1}{6} \left[ \frac{9y^2}{2} - \frac{y^4}{4} \right]_{0}^{3} $$ Substituting the upper limit $y=3$: $$ \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{9(3^2)}{2} - \frac{3^4}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{81}{2} - \frac{81}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{162 - 81}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \left( \frac{81}{4} \right) = \frac{81}{24} $$ Simplifying the fraction: $$ \frac{81}{24} = \frac{27}{8} $$ Final Answer: $$ \iiint_E z \, dV = \frac{27}{8} $$ --- The final answer is $\boxed{\frac{27}{8}}$. --- No graphical results.
