# Problem Statement The goal is to find the volume of the solid enclosed by: 1. The cylinder $x^2 + z^2 = 4$. 2. The plane $y = -1$. 3. The plane $y + z = 4$. # Step 1: Analyze the Geometry The cylinder $x^2 + z^2 = 4$ has its axis along the $y$-axis and a radius of $R=2$. The cross-sections perpendicular to the $y$-axis are circles in the $xz$-plane. The two planes are: - $y = -1$: A plane perpendicular to the $y$-axis. - $y = 4 - z$: A slanted plane. To find the volume, it is most convenient to use cylindrical coordinates where the cylinder's axis is the central axis. However, since the cylinder's equation involves $x$ and $z$, we will set: $$ \begin{cases} x = r \cos \theta \\ z = r \sin \theta \\ y = y \end{cases} $$ The differential volume element in these "cylindrical" coordinates (with $y$ as the height variable) is $dV = r \, dr \, d\theta \, dy$. The bounds for the variables are: - For the cylinder $x^2 + z^2 = 4$, we have $0 \le r \le 2$ and $0 \le \theta < 2\pi$. - For $y$, the boundaries are given by the planes $y = -1$ and $y = 4 - z$. - Substituting $z = r \sin \theta$ into the second plane's equation, we get $y = 4 - r \sin \theta$. Thus, the bounds for $y$ are $-1 \le y \le 4 - r \sin \theta$. # Step 2: Set Up the Triple Integral The volume $V$ is given by: $$ V = \iiint_D dV = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{2} \int_{-1}^{4 - r \sin \theta} r \, dy \, dr \, d\theta $$ # Step 3: Evaluate the Integral First, integrate with respect to $y$: $$ \int_{-1}^{4 - r \sin \theta} r \, dy = r [y]_{-1}^{4 - r \sin \theta} = r(4 - r \sin \theta - (-1)) = r(5 - r \sin \theta) = 5r - r^2 \sin \theta $$ Next, integrate with respect to $r$: $$ \int_{0}^{2} (5r - r^2 \sin \theta) \, dr = \left[ \frac{5}{2}r^2 - \frac{1}{3}r^3 \sin \theta \right]_{0}^{2} = \left( \frac{5}{2}(4) - \frac{1}{3}(8) \sin \theta \right) - 0 = 10 - \frac{8}{3} \sin \theta $$ Finally, integrate with respect to $\theta$: $$ V = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \left( 10 - \frac{8}{3} \sin \theta \right) \, d\theta = \left[ 10\theta + \frac{8}{3} \cos \theta \right]_{0}^{2\pi} $$ $$ V = \left( 10(2\pi) + \frac{8}{3} \cos(2\pi) \right) - \left( 10(0) + \frac{8}{3} \cos(0) \right) $$ $$ V = 20\pi + \frac{8}{3} - \frac{8}{3} = 20\pi $$ # Final Answer The volume of the solid is: $$ \\boxed{20\pi} $$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
