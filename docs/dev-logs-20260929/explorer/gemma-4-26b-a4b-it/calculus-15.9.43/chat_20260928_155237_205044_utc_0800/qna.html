# Problem Solution: Drawing a Silo The problem asks us to use a graphing device to draw a silo consisting of a **cylinder with radius 3 and height 10**, surmounted by a **hemisphere**. ## 1. Mathematical Modeling To draw this using a graphing device, we first define the geometric components using mathematical equations. ### A. The Cylinder (Base) A cylinder of radius $r=3$ and height $h=10$ centered along the $z$-axis can be described as: - **Equation:** $x^2 + y^2 = 3^2$ (or $x^2 + y^2 = 9$) - **Range for $z$:** $0 \le z \le 10$ - **Parametric Equations:** $$ \begin{cases} x = 3 \cos(\theta) \\ y = 3 \sin(\theta) \\ z = z \end{cases} $$ where $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$ and $z \in [0, 10]$. ### B. The Hemisphere (Cap) A hemisphere of radius $r=3$ placed on top of the cylinder (starting at $z=10$) is centered at the point $(0, 0, 10)$. - **Equation:** $x^2 + y^2 + (z - 10)^2 = 3^2$ (or $x^2 + y^2 + (z - 10)^2 = 9$) - **Range for $z$:** $10 \le z \le 13$ - **Parametric Equations:** $$ \begin{cases} x = 3 \sin(\phi) \cos(\theta) \\ y = 3 \sin(\phi) \sin(\theta) \\ z = 10 + 3 \cos(\phi) \end{cases} $$ where $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$ and $\phi \in [0, \pi/2]$. Note that at the boundary $z=10$, both surfaces satisfy $x^2 + y^2 = 9$, meaning they connect smoothly. ## 2.
