# Solution ## Setting Up the Region $E$ is in the first octant ($x,y,z \ge 0$) with $x^2+y^2 \le 1$, bounded below by $z=0$ and above by $z=y$ (from the plane $y=z$). The projection of $E$ onto the $xy$-plane is the quarter disk $x^2+y^2\le 1$, $x\ge0$, $y\ge0$, as shown in the figures above. So with the order $dz\,dx\,dy$: $$ E:\quad 0\le y\le 1,\quad 0\le x\le \sqrt{1-y^2},\quad 0\le z\le y $$ with density $\rho(x,y,z)=1+x+y+z$. ## (a) The Mass $$ m=\int_0^1\int_0^{\sqrt{1-y^2}}\int_0^y (1+x+y+z)\,dz\,dx\,dy $$ Integrating in $z$: $$ \int_0^y (1+x+y+z)\,dz = y(1+x+y)+\tfrac{y^2}{2} $$ Integrating in $x$ over $[0,\sqrt{1-y^2}]$ (verified with SymPy): $$ \int_0^{\sqrt{1-y^2}}\!\!\left[y(1+x+y)+\tfrac{y^2}{2}\right]dx =\frac{3y}{2}\sqrt{1-y^2}+\frac{3y^2}{2}\sqrt{1-y^2}+\frac{y(1-y^2)}{2} $$ Integrating in $y$ from 0 to 1 (using $\int_0^1 y\sqrt{1-y^2}\,dy=\tfrac13$ and $\int_0^1 y^2\sqrt{1-y^2}\,dy=\tfrac\pi{16}$): $$ m = \frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32} $$ ## (b) The Center of Mass **Moment about the $yz$-plane ($M_{yz}$, for $\bar x$):** the $x$-integral gives $\tfrac12 y(1-y^2)\{2+y+(1-y^2)/2\}$, so $$ M_{yz}=\int_0^1 \frac{y(1-y^2)}{2}\left(2+y+\frac{1-y^2}{2}\right)dy=\frac{7}{24} $$ **Moment about the $xz$-plane ($M_{xz}$, for $\bar y$):** the $x$-integral gives $\tfrac12(1-y^2)\{(1+2y)y+(1-y^2)/3\}$, so $$ M_{xz}=\int_0^1 \frac{1-y^2}{2}\left(y+2y^2+\frac{1-y^2}{3}\right)dy=\frac{4}{15}+\frac{\pi}{16} $$ **Moment about the $xy$-plane ($M_{xy}$, for $\bar z$):** the $z$-integral gives $y^2(1+x+y)+\tfrac{y^3}{2}$, so $$ M_{xy}=\int_0^1\int_0^{\sqrt{1-y^2}}\left[y^2(1+x+y)+\tfrac{y^3}{2}\right]dx\,dy=\frac{13}{90}+\frac{\pi}{32} $$ The center of mass $\left(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z\right)=\left(M_{yz}/m,\,M_{xz}/m,\,M_{xy}/m\right)$ is: $$ \bar{x}=\frac{28}{44+9\pi},\qquad \bar{y}=\frac{\frac{\pi}{16}+\frac{4}{15}}{\frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32}},\qquad \bar{z}=\frac{\frac{\pi}{32}+\frac{13}{90}}{\frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32}} $$ ## (c) The Moment of Inertia about the $z$-axis $$ I_z=\iiint_E (x^2+y^2)\,\rho\,dV =\int_0^1\int_0^{\sqrt{1-y^2}}\int_0^y (x^2+y^2)(1+x+y+z)\,dz\,dx\,dy $$ - The $z$-integral gives $(x^2+y^2)\bigl[y(1+x+y)+\tfrac{y^2}{2}\bigr]$. - The $x$-integral gives $\tfrac{y}{6}(1-y^2)\left(2+3y+\tfrac32 y^2\right) + \tfrac{y^2}{12}(1-y^2)^{3/2}\left(8+9y\right)$. - Integrating in $y$ from 0 to 1 (verified with SymPy): $$ I_z=\frac{\pi}{16}+\frac{17}{60} $$ ## Summary of Exact Answers $$ \boxed{m=\frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32}} $$ $$ \boxed{\left(\bar{x},\,\bar{y},\,\bar{z}\right)=\left(\frac{28}{44+9\pi},\;\frac{\frac{\pi}{16}+\frac{4}{15}}{\frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32}},\;\frac{\frac{\pi}{32}+\frac{13}{90}}{\frac{11}{24}+\frac{3\pi}{32}}\right)} $$ $$ \boxed{I_z=\frac{\pi}{16}+\frac{17}{60}} $$ --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./pgfplots_0.tex)
