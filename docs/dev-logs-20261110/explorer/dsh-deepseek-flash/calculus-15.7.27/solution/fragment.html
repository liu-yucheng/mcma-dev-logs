Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 27

The Solid of an Iterated Integral

Problem

Sketch the solid whose volume the iterated integral gives. Then find the volume.

\[ \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-x} \int_0^{2-2z} dy \, dz \, dx \]

Reading the solid

The integral fixes three pairs of bounds. The outer pair is \(0 \le x \le 1\). At a fixed value of \(x\), the middle pair is \(0 \le z \le 1 - x\). At fixed \(x\) and \(z\), the inner pair is \(0 \le y \le 2 - 2z\). The solid \(E\) is the set of points that satisfies all three pairs:

\[ E = \{(x,y,z) : 0 \le x \le 1,\quad 0 \le z \le 1 - x,\quad 0 \le y \le 2 - 2z\}. \]

The outer pair contains no \(y\). It gives the projection \(T\) of \(E\) onto the \(xz\)-plane. The projection is the triangle under the line \(z = 1 - x\):

\[ T = \{(x,z) : 0 \le x \le 1,\quad 0 \le z \le 1 - x\}. \]

The inner pair gives a segment parallel to the \(y\)-axis at each point of \(T\). The segment starts on the plane \(y = 0\) and ends on the plane \(y = 2 - 2z\). Its length depends on \(z\) alone. The length is 2 on the floor \(z = 0\), 1 at \(z = 0.5\), and 0 at \(z = 1\), so the solid is a pyramid that tapers toward the point \((0,0,1)\).

Five planes bound the solid. The next table lists each face, its equation, and the part of the plane that belongs to \(E\). The last two rows give the projections.

The floor is a rectangle, and the other four faces are triangles that meet at the single point \((0,0,1)\). Hence \(E\) is the pyramid with that rectangular base and that apex. The apex is not above the center of the base, so the pyramid is oblique.

Solution

Step 1 — Justify the bounds in the order of the integral

The integral integrates \(y\) first, then \(z\), then \(x\). The outer pair gives the projection \(T\) on the \(xz\)-plane. At each point of \(T\), the middle pair gives the interval \(0 \le z \le 1 - x\). This interval is not empty because \(x \le 1\). The inner pair then gives the segment \(0 \le y \le 2 - 2z\). This segment is not empty because \(z \le 1\) on \(T\). Every point of \(T\) therefore carries a segment, and the three pairs describe the solid \(E\) with no wasted region.

Step 2 — Write the volume integral

The volume of \(E\) is the integral of 1 over \(E\). The bounds of Step 1 place the three differentials in the given order, so

\[ V = \iiint_E dV = \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-x} \int_0^{2-2z} dy \, dz \, dx. \]

The page returns to this integral below, and the numeric scripts confirm its value.

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to \(y\)

The integrand is 1, so the inner integral is the length of the segment:

\[ \int_0^{2-2z} dy = 2 - 2z. \]

The length does not depend on \(x\). The triple integral is now a double integral over \(T\):

\[ V = \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-x} (2 - 2z) \, dz \, dx. \]

Step 4 — Integrate with respect to \(z\)

The antiderivative of \(2 - 2z\) is \(2z - z^2\). At the upper limit \(z = 1 - x\) the antiderivative is

\[ 2(1 - x) - (1 - x)^2 = 2 - 2x - (1 - 2x + x^2) = 1 - x^2. \]

The value at the lower limit \(z = 0\) is 0, so the inner pair of integrals gives

\[ \int_0^{1-x} (2 - 2z) \, dz = \Big[ 2z - z^2 \Big]_0^{1-x} = 1 - x^2. \]

This expression is the area of the slice at the fixed value of \(x\). The slice is a trapezoid. Its parallel sides are 2 on the floor and \(2x\) on the slanted wall, and the distance between the two sides is \(1 - x\). Hence

\[ \frac{1}{2}(2 + 2x)(1 - x) = 1 - x^2, \]

which agrees with the integral. The area falls from 1 at \(x = 0\) to 0 at \(x = 1\).

Step 5 — Integrate with respect to \(x\)

Substitute the result of Step 4 into the outer integral:

\[ V = \int_0^1 (1 - x^2) \, dx = \Big[ x - \frac{x^3}{3} \Big]_0^1 = 1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}. \]

Step 6 — Check the value two other ways

The first check uses the volume formula for a pyramid. The base is the rectangle of area \(B = 2\), and the apex is at height \(h = 1\) above the base plane \(z = 0\). Hence

\[ V = \frac{1}{3} B h = \frac{1}{3} (2) (1) = \frac{2}{3}. \]

The second check sums the horizontal slices of panel (b). The slice at height \(z\) is a rectangle with area \(1 - z)(2 - 2z) = 2(1 - z)^2\), so

\[ V = \int_0^1 2(1 - z)^2 \, dz = \Big[ -\frac{2}{3}(1 - z)^3 \Big]_0^1 = \frac{2}{3}. \]

The scripts repeat the three routes and agree to all printed digits.

Answer

The solid \(E\) is the pyramid with the rectangular base \(0 \le x \le 1\), \(0 \le y \le 2\) on the plane \(z = 0\) and the apex \((0,0,1)\). Its volume is

\[ V = \int_0^1 \int_0^{1-x} \int_0^{2-2z} dy \, dz \, dx = \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.667. \]

Notation
