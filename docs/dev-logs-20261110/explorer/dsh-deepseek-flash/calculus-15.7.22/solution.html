Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 22

A Cylinder Cut by a Slanted Plane

Problem

Use a triple integral to find the volume of the solid that the cylinder x2+z2=4 and the planes y=−1 and y+z=4 enclose.

Reading the solid

The equation of the cylinder does not contain y. The cylinder therefore runs parallel to the y-axis, and its cross-section in the xz-plane is the disk

D={(x,z):x2+z2≤4},

with radius 2 and area 4π=12.566371.

The plane y+z=4 is the roof of the solid: y=4−z. On the disk the variable z runs from −2 to 2, so the roof height 4−z runs from 6 at z=−2 down to 2 at z=2. The floor is the plane y=−1. The smallest roof height is 2 and the floor height is −1, so the roof lies above the floor over the whole disk. The planes do not remove any part of the cylinder; they only give it a slanted top.

The solid is the set of points

E={(x,y,z):x2+z2≤4, −1≤y≤4−z}.

Solution

Step 1 — Integrate along the axis of the cylinder

Fix a point (x,z) of the disk. The segment of the solid through that point starts at the floor and ends at the roof. Its length is

(4−z)−(−1)=5−z.

The length is 7 at z=−2, 5 at z=0, and 3 at z=2. The volume is the integral of this length over the disk:

V=∬D​(5−z)dA.

Step 2 — Use polar coordinates in the disk

Set x=rcosθ and z=rsinθ. Then x2+z2=r2 and dA=rdrdθ. The disk is described by 0≤r≤2 and 0≤θ≤2π, so

V=∫02π​∫02​(5−rsinθ)rdrdθ.

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to r

The angle θ is constant during the inner integration, so

∫02​(5r−r2sinθ)dr=[25r2​−3r3​sinθ]02​=10−38​sinθ.

Step 4 — Integrate with respect to θ

The integral of sinθ over a full period is zero:

V=∫02π​(10−38​sinθ)dθ=20π−38​⋅0=20π.

Step 5 — Check the result by symmetry

The disk is symmetric about the line z=0, and z changes sign under the reflection in that line. Hence the integral of z over the disk is zero, and the volume is the constant height 5 times the area of the disk:

V=∬D​5dA−∬D​zdA=5⋅4π−0=20π.

The symbolic script evaluates the two integrals separately and obtains 20π and 0, in agreement with the iterated integral.

Answer

V=∬D​(5−z)dA=20π≈62.832.

Notation

SymbolMeaning
EThe solid region of integration: the set of points between the floor and the roof inside the cylinder.
DThe disk x2+z2≤4 in the xz-plane. It is the projection of E onto that plane.
dAThe area element of a plane region. Here dA=dxdz in Cartesian coordinates and dA=rdrdθ in polar coordinates.
r,θPolar coordinates in the xz-plane: r is the distance from the origin and θ is the angle from the positive x-axis toward the positive z-axis.
the factor rThe Jacobian factor of polar coordinates. A small polar rectangle has area rdrdθ, not drdθ.
5−zThe length of the segment of E above the point (x,z) of D. It is the roof height minus the floor height.
∬D​The double integral over the plane region D. It adds the values of the integrand over small pieces dA of the region.
πThe ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter, π≈3.14159.

Verification

Three independent computations support the result.

  • part1_step1.py confirms that the roof stays above the floor: the roof heights at z=−2,−1,0,1,2 are 6,5,4,3,2, above the floor −1. The same script estimates the volume by Monte Carlo sampling with the value 62.79188, against 20π=62.83185. The deviation is −1.44 standard errors, and the standard error is 0.02779.
  • part1_step2.py repeats the whole computation with SymPy. The inner integral is 10−8sinθ/3, the outer integral is 20π, and the decimal value is 62.831853071796. The script also evaluates the two terms of Step 5 separately and obtains 20π and 0.
  • part1_step3.py evaluates the volume with mpmath in two different orders of integration. Both orders give 62.83185307179586, which agrees with 20π to all printed digits.
