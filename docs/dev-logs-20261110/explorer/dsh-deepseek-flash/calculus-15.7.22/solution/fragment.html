Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 22

A Cylinder Cut by a Slanted Plane

Problem

Use a triple integral to find the volume of the solid that the cylinder \(x^{2} + z^{2} = 4\) and the planes \(y = -1\) and \(y + z = 4\) enclose.

Reading the solid

The equation of the cylinder does not contain \(y\). The cylinder therefore runs parallel to the \(y\)-axis, and its cross-section in the \(xz\)-plane is the disk

\[ D = \{(x,z) : x^{2} + z^{2} \le 4\}, \]

with radius 2 and area \(4\pi = 12.566371\).

The plane \(y + z = 4\) is the roof of the solid: \(y = 4 - z\). On the disk the variable \(z\) runs from \(-2\) to \(2\), so the roof height \(4 - z\) runs from \(6\) at \(z = -2\) down to \(2\) at \(z = 2\). The floor is the plane \(y = -1\). The smallest roof height is 2 and the floor height is \(-1\), so the roof lies above the floor over the whole disk. The planes do not remove any part of the cylinder; they only give it a slanted top.

The solid is the set of points

\[ E = \{(x,y,z) : x^{2} + z^{2} \le 4,\ -1 \le y \le 4 - z\}. \]

Solution

Step 1 — Integrate along the axis of the cylinder

Fix a point \((x,z)\) of the disk. The segment of the solid through that point starts at the floor and ends at the roof. Its length is

\[ (4 - z) - (-1) = 5 - z. \]

The length is \(7\) at \(z = -2\), \(5\) at \(z = 0\), and \(3\) at \(z = 2\). The volume is the integral of this length over the disk:

\[ V = \iint_{D} (5 - z) \, dA. \]

Step 2 — Use polar coordinates in the disk

Set \(x = r\cos\theta\) and \(z = r\sin\theta\). Then \(x^{2} + z^{2} = r^{2}\) and \(dA = r \, dr \, d\theta\). The disk is described by \(0 \le r \le 2\) and \(0 \le \theta \le 2\pi\), so

\[ V = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{2} (5 - r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr \, d\theta. \]

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to \(r\)

The angle \(\theta\) is constant during the inner integration, so

\[ \int_{0}^{2} \left(5r - r^{2}\sin\theta\right) dr = \left[ \frac{5r^{2}}{2} - \frac{r^{3}}{3}\sin\theta \right]_{0}^{2} = 10 - \frac{8}{3}\sin\theta. \]

Step 4 — Integrate with respect to \(\theta\)

The integral of \(\sin\theta\) over a full period is zero:

\[ V = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \left(10 - \frac{8}{3}\sin\theta\right) d\theta = 20\pi - \frac{8}{3} \cdot 0 = 20\pi. \]

Step 5 — Check the result by symmetry

The disk is symmetric about the line \(z = 0\), and \(z\) changes sign under the reflection in that line. Hence the integral of \(z\) over the disk is zero, and the volume is the constant height 5 times the area of the disk:

\[ V = \iint_{D} 5 \, dA - \iint_{D} z \, dA = 5 \cdot 4\pi - 0 = 20\pi. \]

The symbolic script evaluates the two integrals separately and obtains \(20\pi\) and \(0\), in agreement with the iterated integral.

Answer

\[ V = \iint_{D} (5 - z) \, dA = 20\pi \approx 62.832. \]

Notation

SymbolMeaning
\(E\)The solid region of integration: the set of points between the floor and the roof inside the cylinder.
\(D\)The disk \(x^{2} + z^{2} \le 4\) in the \(xz\)-plane. It is the projection of \(E\) onto that plane.
\(dA\)The area element of a plane region. Here \(dA = dx \, dz\) in Cartesian coordinates and \(dA = r \, dr \, d\theta\) in polar coordinates.
\(r, \theta\)Polar coordinates in the \(xz\)-plane: \(r\) is the distance from the origin and \(\theta\) is the angle from the positive \(x\)-axis toward the positive \(z\)-axis.
the factor \(r\)The Jacobian factor of polar coordinates. A small polar rectangle has area \(r \, dr \, d\theta\), not \(dr \, d\theta\).
\(5 - z\)The length of the segment of \(E\) above the point \((x,z)\) of \(D\). It is the roof height minus the floor height.
\(\iint_{D}\)The double integral over the plane region \(D\). It adds the values of the integrand over small pieces \(dA\) of the region.
\(\pi\)The ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter, \(\pi \approx 3.14159\).

Verification

Three independent computations support the result.

  • part1_step1.py confirms that the roof stays above the floor: the roof heights at \(z = -2, -1, 0, 1, 2\) are \(6, 5, 4, 3, 2\), above the floor \(-1\). The same script estimates the volume by Monte Carlo sampling with the value \(62.79188\), against \(20\pi = 62.83185\). The deviation is \(-1.44\) standard errors, and the standard error is \(0.02779\).
  • part1_step2.py repeats the whole computation with SymPy. The inner integral is \(10 - 8\sin\theta/3\), the outer integral is \(20\pi\), and the decimal value is \(62.831853071796\). The script also evaluates the two terms of Step 5 separately and obtains \(20\pi\) and \(0\).
  • part1_step3.py evaluates the volume with mpmath in two different orders of integration. Both orders give \(62.83185307179586\), which agrees with \(20\pi\) to all printed digits.
