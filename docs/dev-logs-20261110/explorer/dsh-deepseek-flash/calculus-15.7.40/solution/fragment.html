Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 40

Mass and Center of Mass of a Solid with a Parabolic Roof Line

Problem

Find the mass and the center of mass of the solid \(E\) with density \(\rho(x,y,z) = 4\). The solid \(E\) is bounded by the parabolic cylinder \(z = 1 - y^{2}\) and by the planes \(x + z = 1\), \(x = 0\), and \(z = 0\).

\[ m = \iiint_{E} 4 \, dV. \]

Reading the solid

The plane \(x + z = 1\) gives \(x = 1 - z\). The plane \(x = 0\) closes the solid on the back. Together they say that the \(x\)-coordinate is trapped between two surfaces:

\[ 0 \le x \le 1 - z. \]

The inequality \(x \ge 0\) is already true, so the upper limit \(1 - z\) must be nonnegative. That condition gives \(z \le 1\). The parabolic cylinder adds the second ceiling:

\[ 0 \le z \le 1 - y^{2}. \]

The number \(1 - y^{2}\) is nonnegative only when \(|y| \le 1\). The projection of \(E\) onto the \(yz\)-plane is therefore the parabolic segment \(D = \{(y,z) : -1 \le y \le 1, \; 0 \le z \le 1 - y^{2}\}\), and the solid is the set

\[ E = \{(x,y,z) : -1 \le y \le 1, \; 0 \le z \le 1 - y^{2}, \; 0 \le x \le 1 - z\}. \]

A second reading is shorter. Hold \(y\) fixed. The section of \(E\) in the plane of constant \(y\) is the right triangle with vertices \((0, y, 0)\), \((1, y, 0)\), and \((0, y, 1 - y^{2})\). Its horizontal leg has length \(1\), its vertical leg has length \(1 - y^{2}\), and its hypotenuse lies on the plane \(x + z = 1\). The triangle shrinks to a point as \(y\) approaches \(\pm 1\).

No coordinate change is needed. The bounds are flat in \(x\), and the volume element is \(dV = dx \, dz \, dy\). The density is the constant \(4\), so the mass is

\[ m = \int_{-1}^{1} \int_{0}^{1-y^{2}} \int_{0}^{1-z} 4 \, dx \, dz \, dy. \]

Solution

Step 1 — Integrate with respect to \(x\)

The density is constant, so the innermost integral is the length of the fibre in the \(x\)-direction:

\[ \int_{0}^{1-z} 4 \, dx = 4(1 - z). \]

Step 2 — Integrate with respect to \(z\)

Put \(a = 1 - y^{2}\) for the height of the triangle. The \(z\)-integral runs from \(0\) to \(a\):

\[ \int_{0}^{a} 4(1 - z) \, dz = 4\left[ z - \frac{z^{2}}{2} \right]_{0}^{a} = 4\left( a - \frac{a^{2}}{2} \right). \]

Now \(a - a^{2}/2 = (1 - y^{2}) - (1 - 2y^{2} + y^{4})/2 = (1 - y^{4})/2\), hence

\[ \int_{0}^{1-y^{2}} 4(1 - z) \, dz = 2\left(1 - y^{4}\right). \]

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to \(y\)

The integrand is even in \(y\), so the integral over \(-1 \le y \le 1\) is twice the integral over \(0 \le y \le 1\):

\[ m = \int_{-1}^{1} 2\left(1 - y^{4}\right) dy = 2\left[ y - \frac{y^{5}}{5} \right]_{-1}^{1} = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot \left(1 - \frac{1}{5}\right) = \frac{16}{5}. \]

The mass is \(16/5 = 3.2\).

Step 4 — Moment about the \(yz\)-plane

The first moment about the \(yz\)-plane weights each point by its distance \(x\) from that plane:

\[ M_{yz} = \iiint_{E} 4x \, dV = \int_{-1}^{1} \int_{0}^{1-y^{2}} \int_{0}^{1-z} 4x \, dx \, dz \, dy. \]

The inner integral is \(2(1 - z)^{2}\). With \(a = 1 - y^{2}\),

\[ \int_{0}^{a} 2(1 - z)^{2} dz = 2\left[ a - a^{2} + \frac{a^{3}}{3} \right] = 2 \cdot \frac{1 - y^{6}}{3}, \]

because \(a - a^{2} + a^{3}/3 = (1 - y^{6})/3\) after the substitution \(u = y^{2}\). Then

\[ M_{yz} = \frac{2}{3}\int_{-1}^{1} \left(1 - y^{6}\right) dy = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \left(1 - \frac{1}{7}\right) = \frac{8}{7}. \]

Step 5 — Moment about the \(xz\)-plane

The solid and the density are unchanged when \(y\) is replaced by \(-y\): the cylinder \(z = 1 - y^{2}\) and the three planes do not distinguish the sign of \(y\). The integrand \(4y\) is odd in \(y\), and the \(y\)-limits are symmetric, so

\[ M_{xz} = \iiint_{E} 4y \, dV = 0. \]

Step 6 — Moment about the \(xy\)-plane

For the moment about the floor, the inner integral is \(\int_{0}^{1-z} 4z \, dx = 4z(1 - z)\):

\[ \int_{0}^{a} 4z(1 - z) \, dz = 4\left[ \frac{z^{2}}{2} - \frac{z^{3}}{3} \right]_{0}^{a} = 4\left( \frac{a^{2}}{2} - \frac{a^{3}}{3} \right). \]

Substitute \(a = 1 - y^{2}\) and expand in powers of \(y\):

\[ \frac{a^{2}}{2} - \frac{a^{3}}{3} = \frac{1}{6} - \frac{y^{4}}{2} + \frac{y^{6}}{3}, \]
\[ M_{xy} = 4\int_{-1}^{1} \left( \frac{1}{6} - \frac{y^{4}}{2} + \frac{y^{6}}{3} \right) dy = 8\left( \frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{21} \right) = 8 \cdot \frac{4}{35} = \frac{32}{35}. \]

Step 7 — Divide each moment by the mass

The coordinates of the center of mass are the moments divided by \(m = 16/5\):

\[ \bar{x} = \frac{M_{yz}}{m} = \frac{8/7}{16/5} = \frac{5}{14}, \qquad \bar{y} = \frac{M_{xz}}{m} = 0, \qquad \bar{z} = \frac{M_{xy}}{m} = \frac{32/35}{16/5} = \frac{2}{7}. \]

The value \(\bar{x} = 5/14 \approx 0.357\) is less than \(1/2\). That is reasonable: the solid is thickest at \(z = 0\), where the fibre runs the full length \(1\), and it narrows to zero thickness at \(z = 1\). The value \(\bar{z} = 2/7 \approx 0.286\) is less than \(1/2\) for the same reason, because the solid carries most of its volume low down.

Answer

\[ m = \frac{16}{5} = 3.2, \qquad (\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}) = \left( \frac{5}{14}, 0, \frac{2}{7} \right) \approx (0.357, 0, 0.286). \]

Notation

SymbolMeaning
\(\iiint_{E} f \, dV\)The triple integral of \(f\) over the solid \(E\); it is the limit of sums of \(f\) times small volumes.
\(E\)The solid region of integration, that is, the set of points that satisfy the stated inequalities.
\(dV\)The volume element. Here \(dV = dx \, dz \, dy\), because the three coordinates are integrated in that order.
\(\rho(x,y,z)\)The density, here the constant \(4\). In this exercise the units are mass per unit volume.
\(z = 1 - y^{2}\)A parabolic cylinder: the parabola \(z = 1 - y^{2}\) in the \(yz\)-plane, translated along the \(x\)-axis.
\(x + z = 1\)A plane that cuts the solid on the front and forms the hypotenuse of every triangular section.
\(M_{yz}, M_{xz}, M_{xy}\)The first moments of mass about the coordinate planes. \(M_{yz} = \iiint x \rho \, dV\) measures the pull toward the \(yz\)-plane.
\(\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}\)The coordinates of the center of mass, \(M_{yz}/m\), \(M_{xz}/m\), \(M_{xy}/m\).
\(a\)Shorthand for the height \(1 - y^{2}\) of the triangular section at a fixed \(y\).

Verification

Four scripts confirm the result.

  • part1_step1.py checks the inequalities and estimates the mass by Monte Carlo sampling of the box \(0 \le x \le 1\), \(-1 \le y \le 1\), \(0 \le z \le 1\). It reports \(3.20015\) against \(16/5 = 3.2\), a deviation of \(0.08\) standard errors.
  • part1_step2.py repeats the mass integral in SymPy and obtains \(16/5\). It also prints the intermediate results \(4 - 4z\) and \(2 - 2y^{4}\).
  • part1_step3.py evaluates the three moments in SymPy and obtains \(8/7\), \(0\), and \(32/35\), hence the center of mass \((5/14, 0, 2/7)\).
  • part1_step4.py integrates in the reverse order, with \(x\) outermost and \(y\) innermost, using mpmath. It confirms \(m = 3.2\), \(M_{yz} = 1.14285714285714\), \(M_{xz} = 0\), and \(M_{xy} = 0.914285714285714\).
