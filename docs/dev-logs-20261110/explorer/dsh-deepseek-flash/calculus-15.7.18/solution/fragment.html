Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 18

A Triple Integral over a Cylindrical Wedge in the First Octant

Problem

Evaluate the triple integral

\[ \iiint_{E} z \, dV, \]

where \(E\) is bounded by the cylinder \(y^{2} + z^{2} = 9\) and the planes \(x = 0\), \(y = 3x\), and \(z = 0\) in the first octant.

Reading the solid

The equation \(y^{2} + z^{2} = 9\) describes a circular cylinder of radius \(3\) whose axis is the \(x\)-axis. A point of this cylinder has distance \(3\) from the \(x\)-axis, whatever its \(x\)-coordinate is. The plane \(z = 0\) and the first octant keep only the part above the \(xy\)-plane, so every cross-section of \(E\) parallel to the \(yz\)-plane is a quarter of the disk \(y^{2} + z^{2} \le 9\).

The plane \(x = 0\) is the back wall. The plane \(y = 3x\) is a vertical plane through the \(z\)-axis; it cuts the \(xy\)-plane along the line \(y = 3x\). The condition \(x \ge 0\), together with the cylinder, limits \(y\) to the interval \(0 \le y \le 3\), because the cylinder meets the \(xy\)-plane at \(y = 3\). The plane \(y = 3x\) then gives \(x \le y/3\), and at the far rim \(y = 3\) this bound is \(x = 1\).

Therefore \(E\) projects onto the triangle

\[ D_{xy} = \{(x,y) : 0 \le y \le 3,\; 0 \le x \le y/3\}, \]

with vertices \((0,0)\), \((1,3)\), and \((0,3)\). Above a point \((x,y)\) of this triangle the solid reaches from the floor \(z = 0\) up to the cylinder wall \(z = \sqrt{9 - y^{2}}\). The top does not depend on \(x\), because the cylinder is parallel to the \(x\)-axis.

Two projections make the limits visible. The left panel below shows \(D_{xy}\), the triangle in which \(y\) runs from \(0\) to \(3\) and \(x\) runs from \(0\) to \(y/3\). The right panel shows the quarter disk in the \(yz\)-plane that gives the height \(z = \sqrt{9 - y^{2}}\) of each slice.

These two pictures give one description of \(E\):

\[ E = \{(x,y,z) : 0 \le y \le 3,\; 0 \le x \le y/3,\; 0 \le z \le \sqrt{9 - y^{2}}\}. \]

Solution

Step 1 — Set up the iterated integral

The description fixes the order \(dz\,dx\,dy\). The inner variable is \(z\), the middle variable is \(x\), and the outer variable is \(y\):

\[ \iiint_{E} z \, dV = \int_{0}^{3} \int_{0}^{y/3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} z \, dz \, dx \, dy. \]

Step 2 — Integrate with respect to \(z\)

The inner integral has the antiderivative \(z^{2}/2\), so

\[ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} z \, dz = \left[ \frac{z^{2}}{2} \right]_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} = \frac{9 - y^{2}}{2}. \]

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to \(x\)

The result of Step 2 depends only on \(y\). The inner limit of the \(x\)-range is \(0\) and the outer limit is \(y/3\), so the \(x\)-integration multiplies by the width \(y/3\):

\[ \int_{0}^{y/3} \frac{9 - y^{2}}{2} \, dx = \frac{9 - y^{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{3} = \frac{y\left(9 - y^{2}\right)}{6} = \frac{9y - y^{3}}{6}. \]

The factor \(1/6\) is the product of the factor \(1/2\) from the \(z\)-integral and the factor \(1/3\) from the width \(y/3\).

Step 4 — Integrate with respect to \(y\)

The outer integral is a polynomial integral:

\[ \int_{0}^{3} \frac{9y - y^{3}}{6} \, dy = \frac{1}{6} \left[ \frac{9y^{2}}{2} - \frac{y^{4}}{4} \right]_{0}^{3}. \]

At \(y = 3\) the bracket is

\[ \frac{9 \cdot 9}{2} - \frac{81}{4} = \frac{162}{4} - \frac{81}{4} = \frac{81}{4}, \]

and at \(y = 0\) the bracket is zero. Hence

\[ \iiint_{E} z \, dV = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{81}{4} = \frac{81}{24} = \frac{27}{8}. \]

Step 5 — Check the result with a second order

Reverse the two outer integrations. A point of \(D_{xy}\) has \(0 \le x \le 1\), and for a fixed \(x\) the other coordinate runs from the line \(y = 3x\) to the rim \(y = 3\):

\[ \int_{0}^{1} \int_{3x}^{3} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{9-y^{2}}} z \, dz \, dy \, dx = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{3x}^{3} \frac{9 - y^{2}}{2} \, dy \, dx = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{2}\left[ 9y - \frac{y^{3}}{3} \right]_{3x}^{3} dx = \frac{27}{8}. \]

Both orders give \(27/8\), so the limits of Step 1 are correct. A third check uses the sections perpendicular to the \(y\)-axis. The section at a fixed \(y\) is a rectangle of area

\[ A(y) = \frac{y}{3}\sqrt{9 - y^{2}}, \]

and \(\int_{0}^{3} A(y)\,dy = 3\) is the volume of \(E\). The integral of \(z\) is the same sum weighted by the mean height \(z\) of each section, and that weighted sum returns \(27/8\).

Answer

\[ \iiint_{E} z \, dV = \frac{27}{8} = 3.375. \]

Notation

SymbolMeaning
\(\iiint_{E} f \, dV\)The triple integral of the function \(f\) over the solid region \(E\); it is the limit of sums of \(f\) times small volumes.
\(E\)The solid of integration: the set of points in the first octant that satisfy the three given inequalities.
\(dV\)The volume element. In Cartesian coordinates \(dV = dz\,dx\,dy\), the product of the three coordinate differentials in the chosen order.
\(y^{2} + z^{2} = 9\)A circular cylinder of radius \(3\) about the \(x\)-axis: every point of it is at distance \(3\) from that axis.
first octantThe part of space where \(x \ge 0\), \(y \ge 0\), and \(z \ge 0\).
\(D_{xy}\)The projection of \(E\) onto the \(xy\)-plane, here the triangle \(0 \le y \le 3\), \(0 \le x \le y/3\).
\(\sqrt{9 - y^{2}}\)The height of the cylinder wall above the point \((x,y)\) of the floor; it does not depend on \(x\).
\(A(y)\)The area of the section of \(E\) at a fixed value of \(y\), a rectangle of width \(y/3\) and height \(\sqrt{9 - y^{2}}\).
\(\Delta y\)The thickness of one slice in the figure; the slices approximate \(E\), and the sum of their volumes becomes the integral as \(\Delta y \to 0\).

Three independent computations support the result.

  • part1_step1.py checks the three bounding surfaces and estimates the integral by Monte Carlo sampling in the box \(0 \le x \le 1\), \(0 \le y \le 3\), \(0 \le z \le 3\). The estimate is \(3.36825\) against the exact value \(3.37500\), a deviation of \(-2.22\) standard errors with standard error \(0.00305\). The sampled fraction inside \(E\) is \(0.33279\), close to the ratio \(3/9\) of the volume of \(E\) to the volume of the box.
  • part1_step2.py repeats the evaluation with SymPy. It returns the inner integral \(9/2 - y^{2}/2\), the middle integral \(y(9 - y^{2})/6\), the bracket value \(81/4\) at \(y = 3\), and the total \(27/8 = 3.375000000000\). The section check returns the area \(y\sqrt{9 - y^{2}}/3\) and the volume \(3\).
  • part1_step3.py evaluates the integral with mpmath quadrature at \(25\) digits in two orders, \(dz\,dx\,dy\) and \(dz\,dy\,dx\). Both values are \(3.375\), with a deviation of \(0.0\) from \(27/8\).
