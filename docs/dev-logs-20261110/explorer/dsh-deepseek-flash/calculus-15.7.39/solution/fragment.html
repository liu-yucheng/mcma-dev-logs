Stewart, Calculus: Early Transcendentals · Section 15.7 · Exercise 39

Mass and Center of Mass of a Solid with Constant Density

Problem

Find the mass and the center of mass of the solid \(E\). The density is constant, \(\rho(x,y,z) = 2\). The solid \(E\) lies under the plane \(z = 1 + x + y\) and above the region \(D\) in the \(xy\)-plane. The curves \(y = \sqrt{x}\), \(y = 0\), and \(x = 1\) bound \(D\).

Reading the solid

The region \(D\) is the part of the \(xy\)-plane between the curve \(y = \sqrt{x}\) and the \(x\)-axis, cut off at \(x = 1\). Two descriptions of \(D\) are useful, because the solution uses both orders:

\[ D = \{(x,y) : 0 \le x \le 1,\ 0 \le y \le \sqrt{x}\} = \{(x,y) : 0 \le y \le 1,\ y^{2} \le x \le 1\}. \]

The first description uses vertical slices at a fixed \(x\). The second uses horizontal slices at a fixed \(y\), where the curve \(y = \sqrt{x}\) becomes \(x = y^{2}\).

Above each point of \(D\) the solid starts on the base and stops on the roof plane:

\[ E = \{(x,y,z) : (x,y) \in D,\ 0 \le z \le 1 + x + y\}. \]

The roof height \(1 + x + y\) grows from \(1\) at the origin to \(3\) at the corner \((1,1)\). The density is constant, so the mass is twice the volume of \(E\), and the center of mass is the centroid of \(E\).

Solution

Step 1 — Write the mass as an iterated integral

The mass is the integral of the density over the solid:

\[ m = \iiint_{E} \rho \, dV = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} \int_{0}^{1+x+y} 2 \, dz \, dy \, dx. \]

Step 2 — Integrate with respect to \(z\)

The integrand is constant, so the inner integral gives the height of the fibre:

\[ \int_{0}^{1+x+y} 2 \, dz = 2(1 + x + y). \]

Step 3 — Integrate with respect to \(y\)

Hold \(x\) fixed and integrate over \(0 \le y \le \sqrt{x}\):

\[ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} 2(1 + x + y) \, dy = 2\left[ (1 + x)y + \frac{y^{2}}{2} \right]_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} = 2(1 + x)\sqrt{x} + x. \]

Step 4 — Integrate with respect to \(x\)

Write \(\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}\) and integrate term by term:

\[ m = \int_{0}^{1} \left( 2x^{1/2} + 2x^{3/2} + x \right) dx = 2\left( \frac{2}{3} \right) + 2\left( \frac{2}{5} \right) + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{4}{3} + \frac{4}{5} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{40 + 24 + 15}{30} = \frac{79}{30}. \]

Step 5 — Set up the three moments

The first moments of the solid about the coordinate planes are

\[ M_{yz} = \iiint_{E} x\rho \, dV, \qquad M_{xz} = \iiint_{E} y\rho \, dV, \qquad M_{xy} = \iiint_{E} z\rho \, dV. \]

Step 6 — Evaluate the moment about the \(yz\)-plane

Insert \(x\rho = 2x\) and integrate over the same fibre:

\[ M_{yz} = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} 2x(1 + x + y) \, dy \, dx = \int_{0}^{1} \left( 2x(1 + x)\sqrt{x} + x^{2} \right) dx. \]

The inner integral gives \(2x\bigl( (1+x)\sqrt{x} + x/2 \bigr)\). Now integrate the two terms:

\[ M_{yz} = 2\left( \frac{2}{5} + \frac{2}{7} \right) + \frac{1}{3} = 2\left( \frac{24}{35} \right) + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{48}{35} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{144 + 35}{105} = \frac{179}{105}. \]

Step 7 — Evaluate the moment about the \(xz\)-plane

Insert \(y\rho = 2y\):

\[ M_{xz} = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} 2y(1 + x + y) \, dy \, dx = 2\int_{0}^{1} \left( \frac{(1+x)x}{2} + \frac{x^{3/2}}{3} \right) dx. \]
\[ M_{xz} = 2\left( \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{15} \right) = 2\left( \frac{15 + 10 + 8}{60} \right) = 2 \cdot \frac{33}{60} = \frac{11}{10}. \]

Step 8 — Evaluate the moment about the \(xy\)-plane

Here the inner integral is \(\int_{0}^{1+x+y} 2z \, dz = (1 + x + y)^{2}\):

\[ M_{xy} = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} (1 + x + y)^{2} \, dy \, dx. \]

The inner integral is a difference of cubes, because \(1 + x + \sqrt{x}\) is the roof height at the top of the fibre:

\[ \int_{0}^{\sqrt{x}} (1 + x + y)^{2} \, dy = \frac{(1 + x + \sqrt{x})^{3} - (1 + x)^{3}}{3}. \]

Expand the difference. The terms \((1+x)^{3}\) cancel, and the remainder is

\[ 3(1 + x)^{2}\sqrt{x} + 3x(1 + x) + x^{3/2}. \]

Divide by \(3\) and integrate from \(0\) to \(1\):

\[ M_{xy} = \int_{0}^{1} \left( (1+x)^{2}x^{1/2} + x(1+x) + \frac{x^{3/2}}{3} \right) dx = \frac{184}{105} + \frac{5}{6} + \frac{2}{15} = \frac{368 + 175 + 28}{210} = \frac{571}{210}. \]

Step 9 — Divide the moments by the mass

\[ \bar{x} = \frac{M_{yz}}{m} = \frac{179/105}{79/30} = \frac{179 \cdot 30}{105 \cdot 79} = \frac{358}{553} \approx 0.6474, \]
\[ \bar{y} = \frac{M_{xz}}{m} = \frac{11/10}{79/30} = \frac{33}{79} \approx 0.4177, \]
\[ \bar{z} = \frac{M_{xy}}{m} = \frac{571/210}{79/30} = \frac{571}{553} \approx 1.0325. \]

Step 10 — Check the centroid

The roof height at the centroid is \(1 + \bar{x} + \bar{y} = 1142/553 \approx 2.0651\), and \(\bar{z}\) is exactly half of it. That value is consistent, because the roof is a plane and the density is constant. The point \((\bar{x},\bar{y})\) lies in \(D\), since \(\bar{y} = 0.4177 \le \sqrt{\bar{x}} = 0.8046\).

Answer

\[ m = \frac{79}{30} \approx 2.6333, \]
\[ (\bar{x},\bar{y},\bar{z}) = \left( \frac{358}{553},\ \frac{33}{79},\ \frac{571}{553} \right) \approx (0.6474,\ 0.4177,\ 1.0325). \]

The density is constant, so the mass is \(2\) times the volume \(79/60\), and the center of mass is the centroid of \(E\).

Notation

SymbolMeaning
\(\rho(x,y,z)\)The mass density, here the constant \(2\). The mass of a small piece of volume \(dV\) is \(\rho \, dV\).
\(E\)The solid of integration, the set of points under the roof and above the base.
\(D\)The base region in the \(xy\)-plane, bounded by \(y = \sqrt{x}\), \(y = 0\), and \(x = 1\).
\(dV\)The volume element, \(dV = dz \, dy \, dx\) in Cartesian coordinates.
\(z = 1 + x + y\)The roof plane. It slopes upward in both the \(x\) and the \(y\) direction.
\(y = \sqrt{x}\)The curved boundary of \(D\), the upper half of the parabola \(x = y^{2}\).
\(m\)The mass of \(E\), \(m = \iiint_{E} \rho \, dV\).
\(M_{yz}, M_{xz}, M_{xy}\)The first moments about the \(yz\), \(xz\), and \(xy\) planes. Each moment weights a point by its distance from the plane.
\((\bar{x},\bar{y},\bar{z})\)The center of mass, \(\bar{x} = M_{yz}/m\), \(\bar{y} = M_{xz}/m\), \(\bar{z} = M_{xy}/m\).

Verification

Three independent computations support the result.

  • part1_step1.py integrates the mass in the order \(dx\,dy\) and in the order \(dy\,dx\). Both orders give \(79/30 = 2.633333333333\), the volume is \(79/60\), and the mean roof height over \(D\) is \(79/40\).
  • part1_step2.py evaluates the three moments with SymPy and obtains \(M_{yz} = 179/105 = 1.704761904762\), \(M_{xz} = 11/10 = 1.100000000000\), and \(M_{xy} = 571/210 = 2.719047619048\). It also checks that \(m(\bar{x},\bar{y},\bar{z})\) equals the moment vector and that \(\bar{z}\) is half the roof height at \((\bar{x},\bar{y})\).
  • part1_step3.py recomputes the mass and the moments with mpmath quadrature. The values agree with the fractions, and every difference is \(0.0\). A Monte Carlo run over the box \([0,1] \times [0,1] \times [0,3]\) accepts \(1753174\) points and gives mass \(2.62976\) and centroid \((0.64748 \pm 0.00019,\ 0.41799 \pm 0.00019,\ 1.03246 \pm 0.00049)\).
