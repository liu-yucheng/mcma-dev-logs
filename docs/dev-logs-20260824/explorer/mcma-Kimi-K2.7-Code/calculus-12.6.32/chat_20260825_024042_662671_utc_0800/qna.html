We are asked to reduce the equation $$y^2=x^2+4z^2+4$$ to a standard form, classify the surface, and sketch it. ## Step 1: Move all terms to one side Subtract $x^2$, $4z^2$, and $4$ from both sides: $$y^2-x^2-4z^2=4.$$ ## Step 2: Divide by 4 to make the right-hand side 1 $$\frac{y^2}{4}-\frac{x^2}{4}-\frac{z^2}{1}=1.$$ This can also be written as $$\frac{y^2}{2^2}-\frac{x^2}{2^2}-\frac{z^2}{1^2}=1.$$ ## Step 3: Identify the surface The standard form of a **hyperboloid of two sheets** is $$\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}-\frac{z^2}{c^2}=1 \quad\text{or}\quad \frac{z^2}{c^2}-\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1,$$ where two variables have negative coefficients and one has a positive coefficient, giving two separate sheets. Our equation has $y^2$ positive and both $x^2$ and $z^2$ negative. Therefore the surface is a **hyperboloid of two sheets** whose axis of symmetry is the $y$-axis. ## Step 4: Key features - **Vertices:** Set $x=0$ and $z=0$, giving $y^2=4$, so $y=\pm 2$.
