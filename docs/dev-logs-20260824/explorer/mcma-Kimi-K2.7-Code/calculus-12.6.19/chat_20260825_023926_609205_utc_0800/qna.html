We are asked to use traces to sketch and identify the surface $$y = z^2 - x^2.$$ ## Finding the traces A **trace** is the intersection of the surface with a plane parallel to one of the coordinate planes. ### 1. Horizontal traces: $y = k$ Set $y=k$. Then $$z^2 - x^2 = k.$$ This is the equation of a hyperbola in the $xz$-plane (except when $k=0$). - If $k>0$: hyperbola opening along the $z$-axis. - If $k<0$: rewrite as $x^2 - z^2 = -k$, a hyperbola opening along the $x$-axis. - If $k=0$: $z^2 - x^2 = 0$, so $z = \pm x$, two intersecting straight lines. Thus every horizontal trace is a hyperbola (or a degenerate pair of lines). ### 2. Traces parallel to the $yz$-plane: $x = k$ Set $x=k$. Then $$y = z^2 - k^2.$$ This is a parabola opening in the positive $y$-direction, with vertex at $(k, -k^2, 0)$. ### 3. Traces parallel to the $xy$-plane: $z = k$ Set $z=k$. Then $$y = k^2 - x^2.$$ This is a parabola opening in the negative $y$-direction, with vertex at $(0, k^2, k)$. ## Identifying the surface Since the surface has parabolic traces in two perpendicular directions and hyperbolic traces in the third direction, the surface is a **hyperbolic paraboloid**. It is often called a "saddle surface." Its saddle point is at the origin $(0,0,0)$. The key signature is: $$y = \frac{z^2}{b^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2},$$ which is the standard form of a hyperbolic paraboloid opening along the $y$-axis. Here $a=b=1$. $$\boxed{\text{The surface } y = z^2 - x^2 \text{ is a hyperbolic paraboloid (saddle surface).}}$$ --- --- [PGFplots / tikz plot.](./20260825_024132_363601_utc_0800_pgfplots_0.tex)
