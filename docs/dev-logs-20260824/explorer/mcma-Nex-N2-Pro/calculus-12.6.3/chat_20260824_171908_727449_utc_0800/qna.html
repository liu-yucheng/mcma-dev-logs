Answer
The equation is $$ x^2+z^2=1. $$ Since the variable $y$ does not appear, the condition only restricts $x$ and $z$. In every plane $y=k$, the trace is the same circle $$ x^2+z^2=1 $$ in the $xz$-plane. Therefore the surface is the lateral surface of a circular cylinder of radius $1$ whose axis is the $y$-axis.
