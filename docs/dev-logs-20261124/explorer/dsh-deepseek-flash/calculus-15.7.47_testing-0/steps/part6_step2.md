## Step 6.2. Write the answer

### Reason
The question asks for the setup of three integrals, so the answer gives the three integrals with their limits and the value of each one. The center of mass is the quotient of each first moment by the mass.

### Computation
The mass integral, the three first moments, and the moment of inertia all use the limits of Part 1.

### Result
$$m = {{p3_mass}}, \qquad \bar{x} = {{p4_xbar}}, \quad \bar{y} = {{p4_ybar}}, \quad \bar{z} = {{p4_zbar}}, \qquad I_{z} = {{p5_inertia}}.$$
The limits are $-1 \le x \le 1$, $x^{2} \le y \le 1$, and $0 \le z \le 1 - y$.
