Six iterated forms of the volume of a tetrahedron

Express the integral $\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV$ as an iterated integral in six different ways. The solid $E$ is bounded by $x = 2$, $y = 2$, $z = 0$, and $x + y - 2z = 2$.

Part 1: the solid, its volume, and the six orders

Step 1.1: the solid is a tetrahedron of volume 2/3

At $z = 0$ the base is the triangle $x + y \geq 2$ under the lines $x = 2$ and $y = 2$; the slice shrinks to the apex as $z$ grows.

$$ V = \frac{1}{6} \left| \det \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \right| = \frac{2}{3} $$

The exact volume is $2/3$, about 0.667; the Monte Carlo estimate is 0.66504, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the double integral of the top plane over the base triangle with the determinant law and with a Monte Carlo count of 400,000 points, tolerance 0.01.

A reader may place the apex at $(1,1,1)$; the apex is where $x = 2$, $y = 2$, and the slant plane meet, at $(2,2,1)$.

Step 1.2: six orderings, checked symbolically

Each plane gives one bound, and the six inner-to-outer orders follow from solving the slant plane for $z$, $y$, or $x$.

$$ I_1 = I_2 = I_3 = I_4 = I_5 = I_6 = \frac{2}{3} $$

All six nested integrals evaluate to $2/3$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py evaluates every ordering with sympy and checks that each equals the determinant volume $2/3$ exactly.

A reader may write $z = (x+y)/2 - 1$ with the wrong sign; the top plane is $z = (x+y-2)/2$.

Step 1.3: six orderings, checked numerically

Each ordering integrates $1$ over the same tetrahedron.

$$ \left| I_k - \frac{2}{3} \right| < 5 \times 10^{-3}, \qquad k = 1, \dots, 6 $$

All six numeric values land within $5 \times 10^{-3}$ of $2/3$, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py applies a weighted quadrature on a 1501-point grid to each ordering and compares each with the exact value.

The coarse grid underweights the thin tip near $(2,2,1)$; the tolerance absorbs that.

Answer

The volume is $V = 2/3 \approx 0.667$. The vertices of $E$ are $(2,0,0)$, $(0,2,0)$, $(2,2,0)$, and $(2,2,1)$. The six iterated forms are:

  • Ordering 1, inner to outer $dz$, $dy$, $dx$: $$ \int_{0}^{2}\int_{2-x}^{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{x+y-2}{2}} dz\,dy\,dx $$ The inner limit is the top plane.
  • Ordering 2, inner to outer $dz$, $dx$, $dy$: $$ \int_{0}^{2}\int_{2-y}^{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{x+y-2}{2}} dz\,dx\,dy $$ The inner limit is the top plane.
  • Ordering 3, inner to outer $dy$, $dz$, $dx$: $$ \int_{0}^{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{x}{2}}\int_{2-x+2z}^{2} dy\,dz\,dx $$ The inner limit is the slant plane.
  • Ordering 4, inner to outer $dx$, $dz$, $dy$: $$ \int_{0}^{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{y}{2}}\int_{2-y+2z}^{2} dx\,dz\,dy $$ The inner limit is the slant plane.
  • Ordering 5, inner to outer $dy$, $dx$, $dz$: $$ \int_{0}^{1}\int_{2z}^{2}\int_{2-x+2z}^{2} dy\,dx\,dz $$ The outer range ends at the apex height.
  • Ordering 6, inner to outer $dx$, $dy$, $dz$: $$ \int_{0}^{1}\int_{2z}^{2}\int_{2-y+2z}^{2} dx\,dy\,dz $$ The outer range ends at the apex height.

Notes

Four planes bound $E$; the three base planes keep pairs of axes.

The apex $(2,2,1)$ is where $x = 2$, $y = 2$, and the slant plane meet.
