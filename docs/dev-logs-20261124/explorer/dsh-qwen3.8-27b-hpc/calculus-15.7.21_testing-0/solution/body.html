Volume of the Solid Bounded by the Cylinder y = x² and the Planes z = 0 and y + z = 1

Use a triple integral to find the volume of the given solid. The solid is enclosed by the cylinder $y = x^{2}$ and the planes $z = 0$ and $y + z = 1$.

Part 1. The volume by a triple integral

Step 1.1. Set the base region D.

The cylinder $y = x^{2}$ bounds the projection $D$ of the solid onto the $xy$ plane, the plane that keeps the $x$ and $y$ axes.

$$ D: 0 \leq y \leq 1, \quad -\sqrt{y} \leq x \leq \sqrt{y} $$
$$ \int_0^1 2\sqrt{y} \, dy = \frac{4}{3} $$

The area of $D$ is $\frac{4}{3}$.

part1_step1.py prints the exact area $\frac{4}{3}$ and the grid area $1.3328$. The assertion holds.

Reading the cylinder as $x = y^{2}$ swaps the axes and shrinks the base.

Step 1.2. Read the height of the top plane.

At a fixed point $(x, y)$ of $D$, the solid runs from the plane $z = 0$ up to the plane $y + z = 1$, so its height is $1 - y$.

$$ A(y) = 2\sqrt{y}\,(1 - y) $$
$$ \int_0^1 (1 - y) \, dy = \frac{1}{2} $$

The cross-section area is $A(y) = 2\sqrt{y}\,(1 - y)$, and the mean height is $\frac{1}{2}$.

part1_step2.py prints $A(y)$, the exact mean $\frac{1}{2}$, the grid mean $0.49975$, and the Monte Carlo mean $0.49937$.

Treating the height as the constant $1$ ignores the tilt of the plane $y + z = 1$ and inflates the volume.

Step 1.3. Evaluate the triple integral.

Integrate the cross-section area $A(y)$ over $y$. Fubini's theorem keeps the value fixed under a swap of order.

$$ V = \int_0^1 \int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \int_0^{1-y} dz \, dx \, dy = \int_0^1 2\sqrt{y}\,(1 - y) \, dy $$
$$ V = \frac{4}{3} - \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{15} $$

The volume is $V = \frac{8}{15} \approx 0.5333$ cubic units.

part1_step3.py prints the exact value $\frac{8}{15}$, the Fubini value $\frac{8}{15}$, the trapezoid value $0.5333331859$, and the Monte Carlo value $0.5342366667$.

Integrating the height twice, as $(1 - y)^{2}$, adds a wrong power of $y$ and changes the value.

The volume of the solid is $V = \frac{8}{15}$, that is $V \approx 0.5333$ cubic units.

  • The base $D$ lies in the $xy$ plane, the plane that keeps the $x$ and $y$ axes.
  • The region $D$ is the set $0 \leq y \leq 1$, $-\sqrt{y} \leq x \leq \sqrt{y}$.
  • The grid and the Monte Carlo runs check the exact value.
