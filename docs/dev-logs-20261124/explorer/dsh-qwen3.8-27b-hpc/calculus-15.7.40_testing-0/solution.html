Mass and center of mass in the parabolic-cylinder solid

Find the mass and center of mass of the solid $E$ with the density function $\rho$. The solid $E$ is bounded by the parabolic cylinder $z = 1 - y^2$ and the planes $x + z = 1$, $x = 0$, and $z = 0$; take $\rho(x, y, z) = 4$.

Part 1: mass and center of mass

Step 1.1: the bounds and the volume

At height $z$ the slice keeps the $x$ and $y$ coordinates. The parabola $z = 1 - y^2$ gives $|y| \leq \sqrt{1 - z}$, and the plane $x + z = 1$ gives $x \leq 1 - z$. The height runs $0 \leq z \leq 1$.

$$ V = \int_0^1 \int_{-\sqrt{1 - z}}^{\sqrt{1 - z}} \int_0^{1 - z} dx \, dy \, dz = \frac{4}{5} $$

The volume is $4/5 = 0.800000$ and the grid volume is $0.805292$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the exact volume with a $120^3$ midpoint grid and prints the section corners at $z = 0$, $0.25$, and $0.75$.

A reader may bound $y$ by $1$ and integrate a box, but the parabola keeps $|y| \leq \sqrt{1 - z}$.

Step 1.2: the mass and the moments

The density is the constant $4$, so the mass is $4V$ and each moment keeps the coordinate the named plane holds fixed.

$$ M = \frac{16}{5}, \quad M_{yz} = \frac{8}{7}, \quad M_{xz} = 0, \quad M_{xy} = \frac{32}{35} $$

The grid moments are $1.155776$, $0$, and $0.921864$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares each exact moment with the midpoint grid.

A reader may miss the symmetry and expect a nonzero $M_{xz}$, but $y$ is symmetric about $0$.

Step 1.3: the center of mass

Each coordinate of the center of mass is its moment divided by the mass.

$$ (\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}) = \left(\frac{5}{14}, 0, \frac{2}{7}\right) = (0.357143, 0.000000, 0.285714) $$

The Monte Carlo estimate is $(0.357663, 0.000530, 0.285067)$ with $120158$ kept points, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the exact center with a rejection Monte Carlo estimator on $300000$ samples.

A reader may center $y$ at the parabola axis $1$ and lose the symmetry about $y = 0$.

Answer

The mass is $M = 16/5 = 3.200000$. The center of mass is $(5/14, 0, 2/7) = (0.357143, 0.000000, 0.285714)$.

Notes

  • The slice at height $z$ is a rectangle in the $xy$-plane with width $2\sqrt{1 - z}$.
  • The solid is symmetric in $y$, so the center keeps $y = 0$.
  • The plane $x + z = 1$ tilts the front face, so the center keeps $x = 0$.
