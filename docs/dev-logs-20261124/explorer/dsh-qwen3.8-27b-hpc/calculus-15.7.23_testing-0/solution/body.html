15.7.23 The Wedge Cut from the Cylinder

Express the volume of the wedge in the first octant that is cut from the cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 1$ by the planes $y = x$ and $x = 1$ as a triple integral. Then use a computer algebra system to find the exact value of that integral.
ItemContent
GivenThe cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 1$; the planes $y = x$ and $x = 1$; the first octant.
Find(a) The volume as a triple integral. (b) Its exact value.
SymbolMeaningUnit
$x, y, z$Coordinates of a point in the wedgelength
$A(x)$Area of the slice of the wedge at fixed $x$area
$V$Volume of the wedgelength cubed

Part 1: The triple integral

Step 1.1: The slice at fixed x

The first octant keeps $x$, $y$, $z$ nonnegative. The plane $y = x$ caps $y$; the cylinder caps $z$ at $sqrt{1 - y^2}$. The wedge is then $0 leq x leq 1$, $0 leq y leq x$, $0 leq z leq sqrt{1 - y^2}$.

$$E = \left\{ (x, y, z) : 0 \leq x \leq 1, \; 0 \leq y \leq x, \; 0 \leq z \leq \sqrt{1 - y^2} \right\}$$

The slice at fixed $x$ lies in the $yz$ plane. The script prints the corner list at $x = 0$, $x = 0.5$, $x = 1$; the top corner at $x = 0.5$ is $(0.5, 0.8660)$.

The script part1_step1.py compares a grid count of that slice with the exact area $A(0.5) = 0.478306$, tolerance $0.04$.

A common slip caps $y$ at $1$ and drops the plane $y = x$.

Step 1.2: The iterated integral

Integrate $1$ from $z = 0$ to $sqrt{1 - y^2}$, then $y$ from $0$ to $x$, then $x$ from $0$ to $1$.

$$V = \int_0^1 \int_0^x \int_0^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} \, dz \, dy \, dx$$

This is the answer of part (a). The slice area is $A(x) = \frac{x\sqrt{1-x^2}}{2} + \frac{\arcsin x}{2}$.

The script part1_step2.py recomputes the value by trapezoid passes in the same order, agreement $5 \times 10^{-4}$.

A common slip caps $y$ at $1$ and reads the cylinder as a box.

Part 2: The exact value

Step 2.1: The CAS antiderivatives

The CAS returns two antiderivatives. Each must differentiate back to its integrand on $(0.1, 0.9)$.

$$\int \sqrt{1 - y^2} \, dy = \frac{y\sqrt{1-y^2}}{2} + \frac{\arcsin y}{2}, \qquad \int x \sqrt{1 - x^2} \, dx = -\frac{(1-x^2)^{3/2}}{3}$$

The finite difference of each antiderivative matches its integrand to $2.6 \times 10^{-10}$.

The script part2_step1.py checks both derivatives on a 17-point grid.

A common slip signs the second term wrong and gets $\frac{(1-x^2)^{3/2}}{3}$.

Step 2.2: Evaluate at the limits

Evaluate the slice integral at its limits. The $x$ integral splits into two terms.

$$V = \int_0^1 \left( \frac{x\sqrt{1-x^2}}{2} + \frac{\arcsin x}{2} \right) dx = \frac{1}{6} + \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2} \right) = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{3}$$

The exact value is $V = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.4521$, length cubed.

The script part2_step2.py compares the value with a Monte Carlo pass of $300000$ points, tolerance $4\sigma$.

A common slip drops the $\frac{1}{6}$ term and reports $\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2}$.

Answer

Part (a): $V = \int_0^1 \int_0^x \int_0^{\sqrt{1 - y^2}} \, dz \, dy \, dx$.

Part (b): $V = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.4521$, length cubed.

Notes

  • The plane $x = 1$ keeps the $y$ and $z$ axes free; the plane $y = x$ keeps the $z$ axis free.
