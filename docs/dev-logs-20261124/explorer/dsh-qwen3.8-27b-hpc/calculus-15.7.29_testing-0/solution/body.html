Six Orderings for the Dome Solid

Express the integral $\iiint_{E} f(x,y,z) dV$ as an iterated integral in six different ways, where $E$ is the solid bounded by the surfaces $y = 4 - x^{2} - 4z^{2}$ and $y = 0$. The six forms are the orders that keep two outer bounds constant.

Part 1. Describe the solid

Step 1.1 Cross sections and volume

Solve the inequalities for the bounds and compute the volume by a stretch map and by sampling.

$$E = \{(x,y,z) : x^{2} + 4z^{2} \leq 4, 0 \leq y \leq 4 - x^{2} - 4z^{2}\}$$ $$V = 2 \int_{-1}^{1} \int_{-\sqrt{1-u^{2}}}^{\sqrt{1-u^{2}}} (4 - 4u^{2} - 4v^{2}) dv du = 4\pi \approx 12.566371$$

The sections at $y = 0$, $y = 1$, $y = 4$ shrink to a point; $V = 4\pi \approx 12.566371$ cubic units.

The script part1_step1.py prints the section half-axes 2.000000, 1.732051, 0.000000 and the Monte Carlo value 12.552896, and asserts the latter against $4\pi$ within 0.06.

A common error treats the dome as a hemisphere of radius 2 and takes $16\pi/3$; the sections are ellipses, not circles.

Part 2. Six symbolic orderings

Step 2.1 Build the six forms

Project $E$ onto a coordinate plane and solve for the inner variable at each fixed outer pair.

$$I_{1} = \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{0}^{4-x^{2}} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}{2}} 1 dz dy dx$$ $$I_{4} = \int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2}} \int_{0}^{4-x^{2}-4z^{2}} 1 dy dz dx$$

Each of the six nested integrals simplifies to $4\pi$.

The script part2_step2.py integrates each inner bound in sympy, evaluates the two outer integrals on a 400-point grid, and asserts each value against $4\pi$ within 0.08.

Solving $4z^{2} \leq 4 - x^{2} - y$ for $z$ gives a factor $\frac{1}{2}$ inside the root; dropping it doubles the width.

Part 3. Numeric check

Step 3.1 Nested trapezoid grids

Evaluate all six forms numerically to guard against an algebra slip.

$$I_{k} = \sum_{i} \sum_{j} w_{i} w_{j} g_{k}(a_{i}, b_{j})$$

On a 500-point grid each form lands within 0.08 of $4\pi \approx 12.566371$.

The script part3_step3.py prints $I_{1}$ through $I_{6}$ and asserts each value against $4\pi$ within the grid tolerance 0.08.

A grid step missing from the weights scales the whole sum by $n$; the printed values expose that error.

Answer. The six iterated forms

All six equal $\iiint_{E} f(x,y,z) dV$ for the integrand $f(x,y,z)$:

  • Order $dz dy dx$: $\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{0}^{4-x^{2}} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}-y}}{2}} f(x,y,z) dz dy dx$.
  • Order $dx dy dz$: $\int_{-1}^{1} \int_{0}^{4-4z^{2}} \int_{-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}}^{\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z) dx dy dz$.
  • Order $dz dx dy$: $\int_{0}^{4} \int_{-\sqrt{4-y}}^{\sqrt{4-y}} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-y-x^{2}}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-y-x^{2}}}{2}} f(x,y,z) dz dx dy$.
  • Order $dy dz dx$: $\int_{-2}^{2} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2}} \int_{0}^{4-x^{2}-4z^{2}} f(x,y,z) dy dz dx$.
  • Order $dy dx dz$: $\int_{-1}^{1} \int_{-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}}}^{\sqrt{4-4z^{2}}} \int_{0}^{4-x^{2}-4z^{2}} f(x,y,z) dy dx dz$.
  • Order $dx dz dy$: $\int_{0}^{4} \int_{-\frac{\sqrt{4-y}}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{4-y}}{2}} \int_{-\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}}^{\sqrt{4-4z^{2}-y}} f(x,y,z) dx dz dy$.

Notes

  • Each order keeps two outer bounds constant.
  • The $z$ bound carries the factor $\frac{1}{2}$ from $4z^{2}$.
  • The volume uses the stretch $x = 2u$, $z = v$ with Jacobian 2.
