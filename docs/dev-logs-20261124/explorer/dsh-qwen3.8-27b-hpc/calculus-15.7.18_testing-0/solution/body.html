Triple integral over a quarter cylinder solid

Evaluate the triple integral $iiint_E z , dV$. The solid $E$ is bounded by the cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$ and the planes $x = 0$, $y = 3x$, and $z = 0$ in the first octant.

Part 1: evaluate the triple integral

Step 1.1: describe the solid by its sections

At fixed $y$ the cylinder keeps $0 leq z leq sqrt{9 - y^2}$ and the plane $y = 3x$ keeps $0 leq x leq y/3$, so $0 leq y leq 3$.

$$ E = \{(x, y, z) : 0 \leq y \leq 3, \ 0 \leq z \leq \sqrt{9 - y^2}, \ 0 \leq x \leq y/3\} $$

The section at $y = 1.5$ has $z$ up to $2.5981$ and $x$ up to $0.5000$; at $y = 3$ the section closes to a point, and the grid area of the quarter disk is $7.128$ against the target $7.069$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the grid area of the quarter disk with the closed form $9\pi/4$.

A reader may take the section as a rectangle with $z$ up to $3$ and lose the cylinder boundary.

Step 1.2: integrate $z$ through the limits

The inner integral of $z$ gives $(9 - y^2)/2$, and the $x$-length at fixed $y$ is $y/3$.

$$ \iiint_E z \, dV = \int_0^3 \int_0^{y/3} \int_0^{\sqrt{9 - y^2}} z \, dz \, dx \, dy = \int_0^3 \frac{y}{3} \cdot \frac{9 - y^2}{2} \, dy = \frac{27}{8} $$

The exact value is $27/8 = 3.375$ and the trapezoid value is $3.375$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the closed form $27/8$ with the trapezoid rule on the same one-variable integral.

A reader may drop the factor $y/3$ from the $x$-length and integrate $(9 - y^2)/2$ alone, which overcounts.

Step 1.3: Monte Carlo confirmation

The box $[0,1] \times [0,3] \times [0,3]$ contains $E$, so a random sample scales the mean by the box volume.

$$ I \approx \frac{\text{Vol}(B)}{N} \sum_{p_i \in E} z_i $$

The Monte Carlo value is $3.3844$ against the exact value $3.375$, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the Monte Carlo estimate with the closed form $27/8$.

A reader may forget the box volume factor and report the raw mean of $z$.

Answer

The triple integral equals $27/8 = 3.375$.

Notes

  • The plane $y = 3x$ involves the $x$ and $y$ axes, and the $z$-axis lies in it.
  • The order $dz$, $dx$, $dy$ keeps every limit a single expression.
  • The Monte Carlo estimate of step 1.3 agrees with $27/8$ inside the tolerance of its assertion.
