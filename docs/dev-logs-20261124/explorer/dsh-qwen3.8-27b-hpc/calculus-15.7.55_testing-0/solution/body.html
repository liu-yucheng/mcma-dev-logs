15.7.55: the region that maximizes a triple integral

Find the region $E$ for which the triple integral of $1 - x^2 - 2y^2 - 3z^2$ is a maximum. Then compute the exact maximum value of the integral.
GivenFind
integrand $g = 1 - x^2 - 2y^2 - 3z^2$(a) the region $E$, (b) the exact maximum
symbolmeaningunit
$x, y, z$coordinateslength
$g$the integrandas $x^2$
$u, v, w$scaled coordinates, $x = u$, $y = v/\sqrt{2}$, $z = w/\sqrt{3}$length
$I$the maximum valueas $g$ times volume

Part (a): the region

Step 1.1: the integrand is positive inside the ellipsoid

The integrand is $g = 1 - (x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2)$, and each point below one raises the integral.

$$ E = \left\{ (x, y, z): x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2 \leq 1 \right\} $$

The region is the ellipsoid with semi-axes $1$, $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py checks the zero set and the sign of $g$ on a sample.

A larger region adds negative volume, so the maximum stops at $g = 0$.

Part (b): the exact maximum

Step 1.2: the change of variables

The scaling $x = u$, $y = v/\sqrt{2}$, $z = w/\sqrt{3}$ maps the ellipsoid to the unit ball, with Jacobian $\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$.

$$ I = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\iiint_{u^2 + v^2 + w^2 \leq 1} (1 - u^2 - v^2 - w^2)\,du\,dv\,dw $$

The exact value is $\frac{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}{5}$, about 3.090, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the exact integral with a Monte Carlo mean over the ball.

The Jacobian is $\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$, not $\sqrt{6}$; the map shrinks volume.

Step 1.3: the exact maximum is $\frac{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}{5}$

The product of the Jacobian and the ball integral gives the value.

$$ I = \frac{4\pi}{5\sqrt{6}} = \frac{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}{5} $$

The maximum is $\frac{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}{5}$, about 3.090, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo sample over the ellipsoid.

The semi-axis of the $z$-direction is $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, not $\frac{1}{3}$.

Answer

The region is the ellipsoid $x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2 \leq 1$, and the exact maximum is $\frac{2\sqrt{6}\,\pi}{5}$, about 3.090.

Notes

  • The integrand vanishes on the ellipsoid surface.
  • The scaling sends the ellipsoid to the unit ball.
  • Every value is printed by a script named in its step.
