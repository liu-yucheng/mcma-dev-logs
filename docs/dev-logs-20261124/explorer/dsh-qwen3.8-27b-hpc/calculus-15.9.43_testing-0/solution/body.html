The silo volume, cylinder plus hemisphere

The silo is a right circular cylinder of radius 3 and height 10 surmounted by a hemisphere of radius 3. Find the solid and its total volume.

Part 1: the volume

Step 1.1: the solid is the union $C cup H$

The cylinder $C$ keeps $x^2 + y^2 \leq 9$ and $0 \leq z \leq 10$. The cap $H$ keeps $x^2 + y^2 + (z - 10)^2 \leq 9$ with $z \geq 10$.

$$ V = V_C + V_H $$

The exact total $108\pi \approx 339.292$ and the Monte Carlo value $340.2851$ at standard error $0.4662$ are printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo count of 200000 points.

A reader may count the cap twice, but $H$ keeps only $z \geq 10$.

Step 1.2: the cylinder volume

The cylinder keeps $0 \leq r \leq 3$ and $0 \leq z \leq 10$ about the $z$ axis.

$$ V_C = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^3 \int_0^{10} r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta = 90\pi $$

The grid count gives $282.175$ over 2257400 cells, and the exact value $90\pi \approx 282.7433$ is printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the closed form with a grid of step $0.05$ at three heights.

A reader may drop the Jacobian $r$, but the area element is $r \, dr \, d\theta$.

Step 1.3: the hemisphere volume

The cap is a half ball of radius $3$ centered at $z = 10$.

$$ V_H = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^3 \rho^2 \sin\phi \, d\rho \, d\phi \, d\theta = 18\pi $$

The grid count gives $57.2049$, and the exact value $18\pi \approx 56.5487$ is printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the closed form with a grid of step $0.05$ and checks the top point $(0, 0, 13)$.

A reader may model the cap as a cone, but the limit is a half sphere.

The solid is the union of the cylinder and the hemisphere, and the total volume is

$$ V = 90\pi + 18\pi = 108\pi \approx 339.292 \text{ cubic units} $$
  • The top point is $(0, 0, 13)$.
  • The grid prints the boundary radius $3.0000$ at three heights.
  • The Monte Carlo error stays under five standard errors.
