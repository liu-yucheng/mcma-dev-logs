15.7.31: Six iterated forms of the triple integral

Express the integral $\iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV$ as an iterated integral in six different ways. The solid $E$ is bounded by the surfaces $y = x^3$, $z = 0$, and $y + 2z = 4$.
GivenFind
$E$ bounded by $y = x^3$, $z = 0$, and $y + 2z = 4$ in the first octant$\iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV$ as an iterated integral in six orders
SymbolMeaningUnit
$x, y, z$coordinates of a point in spacenone
$f(x, y, z)$integrand, fixed but arbitrarynone
$E$solid with $0 \leq x \leq 4^{1/3}$, $x^3 \leq y \leq 4$, $0 \leq z \leq (4-y)/2$none

Part 1: the six iterated orders

Step 1.1: set the x-first order

The walls give $0 \leq x \leq 4^{1/3}$, $x^3 \leq y \leq 4$, and $0 \leq z \leq (4-y)/2$.

$$ \iiint_E f \, dV = \int_0^{4^{1/3}} \int_{x^3}^{4} \int_0^{(4-y)/2} f(x, y, z) \, dz \, dy \, dx $$

For $f = 1$ the volume is $\frac{18 \cdot 4^{1/3}}{7} \approx 4.0819$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo count of $200000$ samples in the box $[0, 4^{1/3}] \times [0, 4] \times [0, 2]$.

A reader may drop the wall $y = x^3$ and take $y$ from $0$ to $4$.

Step 1.2: check all six orders at once

Solve the walls for each variable. For instance, $z$ first gives $y$ from $x^3$ to $4 - 2z$.

$$ \int_0^{4^{1/3}} \int_0^{(4-x^3)/2} \int_{x^3}^{4-2z} f(x, y, z) \, dy \, dz \, dx $$

All six orders give $4.081888$ for $f = 1$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py evaluates all six orders of $f = 1$ exactly and cross-checks with a grid volume $4.0693$ at step $h = 0.05$.

In the $y$-then-$z$ order, $y$ ends at $4 - 2z$, not at $4$.

Step 1.3: the section at each height

At height $z$, the section satisfies $x^3 \leq y \leq 4 - 2z$ with $x \geq 0$.

$$ \begin{aligned} z = 0 &: \; 0 \leq x \leq 2^{2/3}, \; x^3 \leq y \leq 4 \\ z = 1 &: \; 0 \leq x \leq 2^{1/3}, \; x^3 \leq y \leq 2 \\ z = 2 &: \; \text{the point } (0, 0, 2) \end{aligned} $$

The section shrinks to the point $(0, 0, 2)$; corners $(0, 0, 0)$, $(0, 4, 0)$, and $(4^{1/3}, 4, 0)$ print from part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py prints the boundary at three heights, the corner list, and the grid top $z = 1.995$ against the exact top $z = 2$.

A reader may keep a positive section at $z = 2$ instead of the collapsed point.

Answer

The six iterated forms of $\iiint_E f(x, y, z) \, dV$ are:

$$ \int_0^{4^{1/3}} \int_{x^3}^{4} \int_0^{(4-y)/2} f(x, y, z) \, dz \, dy \, dx $$ $$ \int_0^{4^{1/3}} \int_0^{(4-x^3)/2} \int_{x^3}^{4-2z} f(x, y, z) \, dy \, dz \, dx $$ $$ \int_0^{4} \int_0^{\sqrt[3]{y}} \int_0^{(4-y)/2} f(x, y, z) \, dz \, dx \, dy $$ $$ \int_0^{4} \int_0^{(4-y)/2} \int_0^{\sqrt[3]{y}} f(x, y, z) \, dx \, dz \, dy $$ $$ \int_0^{2} \int_0^{4-2z} \int_0^{\sqrt[3]{y}} f(x, y, z) \, dx \, dy \, dz $$ $$ \int_0^{2} \int_0^{\sqrt[3]{4-2z}} \int_{x^3}^{4-2z} f(x, y, z) \, dy \, dx \, dz $$

For $f = 1$ each form equals $\frac{18 \cdot 4^{1/3}}{7} \approx 4.0819$.

Notes

  • The upper limit $x = 4^{1/3}$ follows from the line $y = 4$, $z = 0$ on the plane $y + 2z = 4$.
  • The top $z = 2$ holds on the line $x = 0$, $y = 4$ and shrinks to the point $(0, 0, 2)$.
  • The wall $y = x^3$ keeps the solid in the first octant with $x \geq 0$.
