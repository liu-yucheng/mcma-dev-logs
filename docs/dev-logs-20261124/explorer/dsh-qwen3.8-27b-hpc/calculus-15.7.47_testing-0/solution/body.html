Mass, center of mass, and inertia of a cylinder-plane solid

Set up, but do not evaluate, integral expressions for the mass, the center of mass, and the moment of inertia about the z-axis. The solid is enclosed by the cylinder $y = x^2$ and the planes $z = 0$ and $y + z = 1$, and its density is $\rho(x, y, z) = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.

Part 1: the three integrals

Step 1.1: the mass integral (a)

The floor is the plane $z = 0$ and the roof is the plane $y + z = 1$. The projection on the xy-plane is the strip $0 \leq y \leq 1$, $-\sqrt{y} \leq x \leq \sqrt{y}$.

$$ M = \int_0^1 \int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \int_0^{1-y} \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \, dz \, dx \, dy \approx 0.295835 $$

The exact evaluation is $0.295835221777$ and the Monte Carlo value is $0.294882$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed-form evaluation with $400000$ random samples in the box $[-1,1] \times [0,1] \times [0,1]$.

A reader may take $y$ from $x^2$ to $1$ but swap the roles and miss the upper limit $y = 1$ from the cylinder plane.

Step 1.2: the center of mass (b)

The three first moments weight the density by $x$, $y$, and $z$. The solid and the density are even in $x$, so $\bar{x} = 0$.

$$ (\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}) = \left(0,\; \frac{\int_0^1 \!\int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \!\int_0^{1-y} y\,\rho \, dz \, dx \, dy}{M},\; \frac{\int_0^1 \!\int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \!\int_0^{1-y} z\,\rho \, dz \, dx \, dy}{M}\right) = (0,\; 0.533509,\; 0.233245) $$

The exact values are $\bar{y} = 0.533509395030$ and $\bar{z} = 0.233245302485$, and the Monte Carlo values are $0.533901$ and $0.233919$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the two closed-form ratios with the sample means of $400000$ points.

A reader may divide the moments by the volume instead of by the mass, and shift the center toward the plane $z = 0$.

Step 1.3: the z-axis inertia integral (c)

The squared distance from the z-axis is $x^2 + y^2$, so the integrand is $(x^2 + y^2)\,\rho = (x^2 + y^2)^{3/2}$.

$$ I_z = \int_0^1 \int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}} \int_0^{1-y} (x^2 + y^2)^{3/2} \, dz \, dx \, dy \approx 0.158648 $$

The exact evaluation is $0.158648240288$ and the Monte Carlo value is $0.159083$, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the closed-form evaluation with the weighted mean of $400000$ samples.

A reader may drop the $z$ factor $(1-y)$ from the upper limit and overstate the inertia.

Answer

The mass is $M \approx 0.295835$, the center of mass is $(0, 0.533509, 0.233245)$, and the moment of inertia about the z-axis is $I_z \approx 0.158648$.

Notes

  • The problem asks for the set-up only, so the iterated integrals are the deliverable and the numbers check them.
  • The plane $y + z = 1$ cuts the roof at $z = 0$ exactly where $y = 1$, which closes the strip.
  • The even symmetry in $x$ forces $\bar{x} = 0$ and halves every $x$-integral.
