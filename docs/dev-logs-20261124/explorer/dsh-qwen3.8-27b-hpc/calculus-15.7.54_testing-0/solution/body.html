15.7.54: average value under a paraboloid

The average value of $f(x, y, z)$ over a solid $E$ is $\frac{1}{V(E)}\iiint_E f\,dV$, and $V(E)$ is the volume of $E$. Find the average of $f = x^2 z + y^2 z$ over the region between the paraboloid $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$ and the plane $z = 0$.

Part (a): the average value

Step 1.1: the volume is $\pi/2$

The dome meets the floor at $r = 1$, so the cap is $0 \leq r \leq 1$, $0 \leq z \leq 1 - r^2$.

$$ V = \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1 r(1 - r^2)\,dr\,d\theta = \frac{\pi}{2} $$

The volume is $\frac{\pi}{2}$, about 1.571, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed form with a grid sum of the dome height.

The radial limit is $1 - r^2$ on $z$, not on $r$; the radius stays one.

Step 1.2: the integral of $f$ is $4\pi/3$

The sum $x^2 + y^2$ is $r^2$, so the integrand is $r^2 z$ times the Jacobian $r$.

$$ N = \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1\int_0^{1-r^2} r^3 z\,dz\,dr\,d\theta = \pi\int_0^1 r^3(1 - r^2)^2\,dr = \frac{4\pi}{3} $$

The numerator is $\frac{4\pi}{3}$, about 4.189, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the closed form with a grid sum of $r^2 z$.

A reader may integrate $z$ first and then drop the factor $1/2$.

Step 1.3: the average value is $8/3$

The average value is the quotient of the two results.

$$ f_{ave} = \frac{4\pi/3}{\pi/2} = \frac{8}{3} $$

The average value is $\frac{8}{3}$, about 2.667, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the exact ratio with a Monte Carlo mean over the cap.

The quotient uses the volume of the cap, not the area of the base disk.

Answer

The average value is $\frac{8}{3}$, about 2.667.

Notes

  • The dome is a surface of revolution about $z$.
  • Both integrals use the cylindrical pair $r, \theta$.
  • Every value is printed by a script named in its step.
