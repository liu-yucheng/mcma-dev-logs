Mass, Center of Mass, and Moment of Inertia of the Solid E

Let $E$ be the solid in the first octant bounded by the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and the planes $y = z$, $x = 0$, and $z = 0$. The solid has density $\rho(x, y, z) = 1 + x + y + z$.

Part 1: The exact quantities for E

Step 1.1: the volume and the mass

In polar form the plane $z = y$ gives $0 \leq z \leq r \sin\theta$, and the density reads $1 + r\cos\theta + r\sin\theta + z$.

$$ V = \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^1 \int_0^{r \sin\theta} r \, dz \, dr \, d\theta = \frac{1}{3}, \qquad M = \iiint_E \rho \, dV = \frac{44 + 9\pi}{96} $$

The volume is $1/3$ and the mass is $(44 + 9\pi)/96 \approx 0.7529$, printed by part1_step1.py.

A reader may use $0 \leq z \leq r$ and miss the plane $z = y$.

Step 1.2: the center of mass

Each coordinate divides its first moment by the mass $M$.

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{28}{44 + 9\pi}, \qquad \bar{y} = \frac{2(64 + 15\pi)}{5(44 + 9\pi)}, \qquad \bar{z} = \frac{208 + 45\pi}{15(44 + 9\pi)} $$

The center is $(0.3874, 0.6150, 0.3223)$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the exact first moments with a Monte Carlo weighted average.

A reader may forget the weight $\rho$ inside the first moments.

Step 1.3: the moment of inertia about the z-axis

The lever arm to the $z$-axis is $r$, so the integrand is $r^2 \rho$.

$$ I_z = \int_0^{\pi/2} \int_0^1 \int_0^{r \sin\theta} r^3 (1 + r\cos\theta + r\sin\theta + z) \, dz \, dr \, d\theta = \frac{17}{60} + \frac{\pi}{16} $$

The moment is $17/60 + \pi/16 \approx 0.4797$, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo sample of $r^2 \rho$.

A reader may use $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ and mix in the moment about a point.

Answer

  • Mass: $M = \frac{44 + 9\pi}{96} \approx 0.7529$.
  • Center of mass: $\left( \frac{28}{44 + 9\pi}, \frac{2(64 + 15\pi)}{5(44 + 9\pi)}, \frac{208 + 45\pi}{15(44 + 9\pi)} \right) \approx (0.3874, 0.6150, 0.3223)$.
  • Moment of inertia about the $z$-axis: $I_z = \frac{17}{60} + \frac{\pi}{16} \approx 0.4797$.

Notes

  • The plane $z = y$ caps the solid at the radial height $r \sin\theta$.
  • The volume $V = 1/3$ is printed by part1_step1.py.
  • Each value lists the exact form, then the decimal.
