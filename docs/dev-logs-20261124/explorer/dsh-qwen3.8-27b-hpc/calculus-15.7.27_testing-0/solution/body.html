Part 1: The solid and its volume

Step 1: Read the bounds and list the corner points

Each limit bounds one face. Intersecting the faces gives the corner points.

$$C = \{(0,0,0),\ (1,0,0),\ (1,2,0),\ (0,2,0),\ (0,0,1)\}$$

The solid is a pyramid with a rectangular base in the $z = 0$ plane and apex $(0, 0, 1)$.

The script part1_step1.py solves each triple of face planes, prints the corner list, and compares the pyramid formula $\frac{2}{3}$ with a Monte Carlo value $0.6684$.

Do not call it a tetrahedron. The base has four corners, so the solid has five vertices.

Step 2: Evaluate the iterated integral exactly

Integrate from the inner limit out. Every limit is linear in its own variable.

$$\int_0^{2-2z} dy = 2 - 2z, \qquad \int_0^{1-x} (2-2z) \, dz = 1 - x^2$$
$$V = \int_0^1 (1 - x^2) \, dx = \left[ x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^1 = \frac{2}{3}$$

The exact volume is $V = \frac{2}{3}$, about $0.6667$ cubic units.

The script part1_step2.py runs the sympy integral and checks the section form $\int_0^1 2(1-t)^2 \, dt$ plus a trapezoid grid of $200001$ points.

A common slip is to write $1 - x$ instead of $1 - x^2$ after the $z$ integral.

Step 3: Monte Carlo cross-check

Sample the enclosing box $[0,1] \times [0,2] \times [0,1]$ and count the points inside the solid.

$$\hat{V} = 2 \cdot \frac{N_{in}}{N}, \qquad N = 400000$$

The estimate is $\hat{V} = 0.6668$ with a standard error of $0.0015$.

The script part1_step3.py draws $400000$ samples and asserts that the error stays below $6$ standard errors.

Missing the factor $2$, the box volume, gives $\frac{1}{3}$ instead of $\frac{2}{3}$.

The solid is a pyramid whose base is the rectangle $0 \leq x \leq 1$, $0 \leq y \leq 2$ in the $z = 0$ plane and whose apex is $(0, 0, 1)$.

The volume is $V = \frac{2}{3}$, about $0.6667$ cubic units.

  • Five faces bound the solid: $x = 0$, $y = 0$, $z = 0$, $x + z = 1$, $y + 2z = 2$.
  • Horizontal sections are rectangles of area $2(1-z)^2$, which shrink to a point at $z = 1$.
  • The Monte Carlo standard error is $0.0015$ at $400000$ samples.
