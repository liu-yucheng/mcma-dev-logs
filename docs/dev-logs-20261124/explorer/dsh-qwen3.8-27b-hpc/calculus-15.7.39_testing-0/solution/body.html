Mass and center of mass under the plane $z = 1 + x + y$

Find the mass and center of mass of the solid $E$ with the density function $\rho$. The solid $E$ lies under the plane $z = 1 + x + y$ and above the region in the $xy$-plane bounded by $y = \sqrt{x}$, $y = 0$, and $x = 1$; take $\rho(x, y, z) = 2$.

Part 1: mass and center of mass

Step 1.1: the base $D$ and the mass

The base $D$ keeps the $x$ and $y$ coordinates. It spans $0 \leq x \leq 1$ and $0 \leq y \leq \sqrt{x}$. The plane is above $D$, so $z$ runs from $0$ to $1 + x + y$.

$$ M = \iiint_E 2 \, dV = \int_0^1 \int_0^{\sqrt{x}} 2 (1 + x + y) \, dy \, dx = \frac{79}{30} $$

The area of $D$ is $2/3$, the volume is $79/60 = 1.316667$, the mass is $79/30 = 2.633333$, and the grid mass is $2.633300$, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the exact mass with a midpoint grid of $800^2$ columns and prints the section corners at $z = 0.5$, $1$, and $1.5$.

A reader may take the base as the unit square and miss the curve $y = \sqrt{x}$.

Step 1.2: the moments of $E$

The moment about a plane keeps the coordinate the plane holds fixed, so $M_{yz}$ keeps $x$, $M_{xz}$ keeps $y$, and $M_{xy}$ keeps $z$.

$$ M_{yz} = \frac{179}{105}, \quad M_{xz} = \frac{11}{10}, \quad M_{xy} = \frac{337}{180} $$

The grid moments are $1.704765$, $1.099990$, and $1.872236$, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares each exact moment with the same midpoint grid.

A reader may weight by $z$ in $M_{yz}$, but that plane keeps $x$.

Step 1.3: the center of mass

Each coordinate of the center of mass is its moment divided by the mass.

$$ (\bar{x}, \bar{y}, \bar{z}) = \left(\frac{358}{553}, \frac{33}{79}, \frac{337}{474}\right) = (0.647378, 0.417722, 0.710970) $$

The Monte Carlo estimate is $(0.647252, 0.417217, 0.710588)$ with $266336$ kept points, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py compares the exact center with a weighted Monte Carlo estimator on $400000$ samples.

A reader may sample a box and forget the weight by the column height $1 + x + y$.

Answer

The mass is $M = 79/30 = 2.633333$. The center of mass is $(358/553, 33/79, 337/474) = (0.647378, 0.417722, 0.710970)$.

Notes

  • The base $D$ is the region between $y = 0$ and $y = \sqrt{x}$ for $0 \leq x \leq 1$.
  • The top plane is above the base, so the solid has no hole.
  • The weighted Monte Carlo estimator reuses one draw of the base for every mass term.
