Volume of the Solid from the Iterated Integral

The iterated integral $\int_0^2 \int_0^{2-y} \int_0^{4-y^2} dx \, dz \, dy$ gives the volume of the solid $E$. Describe the cross sections of $E$ and find its exact volume.

Part 1: Sketch the Solid and Find the Volume

Step 1.1: Read the Bounds

The inner limits depend only on $y$. At fixed $y$ the slice is a rectangle in the $xz$ plane.

$$A(y) = (4 - y^2)(2 - y)$$ $y = 0$: $x$ from $0$ to $4$, $z$ from $0$ to $2$, area $8$. $y = 1$: $x$ from $0$ to $3$, $z$ from $0$ to $1$, area $3$. $y = 2$: both bounds are $0$, area $0$.

The slice is a rectangle of width $4 - y^2$ and height $2 - y$. It shrinks to a point at $y = 2$.

part1_step1.py checks the exact area against a grid sum and prints PASS 1.1.

Do not treat the bounds as constants when $y$ changes.

Step 1.2: Evaluate the Integral

Integrate in the order $dx$, then $dz$, then $dy$. sympy gives the exact value. A trapezoid sum checks it.

$$V = \int_0^2 \int_0^{2-y} \int_0^{4-y^2} dx \, dz \, dy = \int_0^2 (4 - y^2)(2 - y) \, dy$$ $$= \int_0^2 (8 - 4y - 2y^2 + y^3) \, dy$$ $$= 16 - 8 - \frac{16}{3} + 4 = \frac{20}{3} \approx 6.666667$$

The exact volume is $V = \frac{20}{3}$ cubic units, about $6.667$.

part1_step2.py matches the sympy value to a trapezoid sum within $10^{-3}$ and prints PASS 1.2.

Do not drop the $y$ from the limit $4 - y^2$ when it becomes the slice area.

Step 1.3: Monte Carlo Cross-Check

Sample uniform points in the box $4 \times 2 \times 2$. Keep the fraction inside $E$. Multiply by the box volume $16$.

$$V_{\text{MC}} = 16 \cdot \hat{p}, \qquad \hat{p} = 0.4175$$ $$V_{\text{MC}} = 6.6806, \qquad \text{standard error} = 0.0176$$

The estimate $6.6806$ lies within one standard error of $\frac{20}{3} = 6.6667$.

part1_step3.py compares the Monte Carlo value to the exact value within $4$ standard errors and prints PASS 1.3.

Do not reuse the box bounds after changing the solid.

Answer

The solid $E$ lies in $0 \le y \le 2$. Its $xz$ slice at height $y$ is the rectangle $0 \le x \le 4 - y^2$ by $0 \le z \le 2 - y$. The exact volume is $V = \frac{20}{3} \approx 6.667$ cubic units.

Notes

  • The outer variable $y$ sweeps the slice from $y = 0$ to $y = 2$.
  • The slice area $A(y) = (4 - y^2)(2 - y)$ falls from $8$ to $0$.
