15.7.22 Volume of the Solid by a Triple Integral

Use a triple integral to find the volume of the given solid. The solid is enclosed by the cylinder $x^2 + z^2 = 4$ and the planes $y = -1$ and $y + z = 4$.

GivenThe solid enclosed by the cylinder $x^2 + z^2 = 4$ and the planes $y = -1$ and $y + z = 4$.

Part 1: The volume of the solid

Step 1.1: The cross section is a disk of radius 2

The cylinder has radius $2$ in the $xz$-plane, so its axis is the $y$-axis. For fixed $y$ the slice is the disk $D$ with area $4\pi$. On $D$ the top plane gives $y = 4 - z$ between $2$ and $6$, always above $y = -1$.

$$A = \iint_D dA = \pi \cdot 2^2 = 4\pi$$

The slice at $y = -1$ is the full disk with corners $(\pm 2, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 2)$. At $y = 6$ it shrinks to the point $(0, -2)$.

The script part1_step1.py compares the exact area $4\pi$ with a grid count on $D$, tolerance $0.02$, and prints the slice at three values of $y$.

A common slip reads the cylinder as $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ with the axis along $z$ and builds the wrong solid.

Step 1.2: Integrate the height over the disk

For each point of $D$ the variable $y$ runs from $-1$ to $4 - z$. The height is $(4 - z) - (-1) = 5 - z$. The volume is the integral of this height over $D$.

$$V = \iint_D (5 - z) \, dA = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^2 (5 - r \sin \theta) \, r \, dr \, d\theta = 20\pi$$

The exact volume is $V = 20\pi \approx 62.8319$ cubic units.

The script part1_step2.py checks the exact value against a grid sum of the height over $D$, tolerance $0.02$.

A common slip integrates the height over the square $[-2, 2] \times [-2, 2]$ and gets $80$ instead of $20\pi$.

Step 1.3: Monte Carlo confirmation

A random pass over the box $-2 \leq x \leq 2$, $-1 \leq y \leq 6$, $-2 \leq z \leq 2$ counts the points inside the solid. The box volume is $4 \cdot 7 \cdot 4 = 112$.

$$V \approx 112 \cdot \frac{\text{hits}}{n}, \qquad \text{hit: } x_i^2 + z_i^2 \leq 4 \text{ and } -1 \leq y_i \leq 4 - z_i$$

With $n = 400000$ points the estimate is $62.797$, within $0.04$ of $20\pi$.

The script part1_step3.py asserts the estimate against $20\pi$ within $0.2$.

A common slip uses the box $-1 \leq y \leq 5$ and clips the solid at $z = -2$, where the top plane reaches $y = 6$.

Answer

The volume is $V = 20\pi \approx 62.8319$ cubic units.

Notes

  • The axis of the cylinder is the $y$-axis, so the disk $D$ stays fixed in the $xz$-plane for every $y$.
  • The top plane reaches $y = 6$ at $z = -2$, so the Monte Carlo box must cover $-1 \leq y \leq 6$.
  • The term $z$ integrates to $0$ over $D$ by symmetry, so $V = 5 \cdot \text{Area}(D) = 20\pi$.
