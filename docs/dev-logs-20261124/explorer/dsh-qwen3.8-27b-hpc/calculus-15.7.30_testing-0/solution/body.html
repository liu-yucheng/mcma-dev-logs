Six iterated forms of the volume of a cylinder segment

Express the integral $\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV$ as an iterated integral in six different ways. The solid $E$ is bounded by $y^2 + z^2 = 9$, $x = -2$, and $x = 2$.

Part 1: the solid, its volume, and the six orders

Step 1.1: the slice at every x is a disk of radius 3

The two planes keep $y$ and $z$, so the slice at each $x$ is the full disk $y^2 + z^2 \leq 9$.

$$ V = 4 \cdot 9\pi = 36\pi $$

The exact volume is $36\pi$, about 113.097; the Monte Carlo estimate is 112.9831, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo count of 200,000 points in the box of side 6, tolerance 0.5.

A reader may take the slice for a semicircle, but the disk is full because the bound has no sign condition.

Step 1.2: six orderings, checked symbolically

The constant $x$ range and the symmetric disk bounds allow six inner-to-outer orders.

$$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} dz\,dy\,dx = 36\pi $$

All six nested integrals simplify to $36\pi$; a trapezoid pass gives 113.097293 against 113.097336, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py verifies each ordering by symbolic simplification and checks one ordering against a 20,000-step trapezoid, tolerance $10^{-3}$.

Swapping $y$ and $z$ changes no bound, because the disk is symmetric in them.

Step 1.3: six orderings, checked numerically

Each ordering is a valid iterated integral of $1$ over $E$.

$$ \left| V_k - 36\pi \right| < 10^{-3}, \qquad k = 1, \dots, 6 $$

All six numeric values land within $10^{-3}$ of $36\pi$, printed by part1_step3.py.

part1_step3.py evaluates all six orderings with nested trapezoid rules and compares each with the exact value.

A coarse grid hides a wrong bound; the tolerance matches the 20,000-step grid.

Answer

The volume is $V = 36\pi \approx 113.097$. The six iterated forms are:

  • Ordering 1, inner to outer $dz$, $dy$, $dx$: $$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} dz\,dy\,dx $$ The $x$ range is constant.
  • Ordering 2, inner to outer $dy$, $dz$, $dx$: $$ \int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} dy\,dz\,dx $$ The $x$ range is constant.
  • Ordering 3, inner to outer $dx$, $dy$, $dz$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}}\int_{-2}^{2} dx\,dy\,dz $$ The $x$ range is constant.
  • Ordering 4, inner to outer $dx$, $dz$, $dy$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}}\int_{-2}^{2} dx\,dz\,dy $$ The $x$ range is constant.
  • Ordering 5, inner to outer $dz$, $dx$, $dy$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-y^2}}^{\sqrt{9-y^2}} dz\,dx\,dy $$ The $y$ range is fixed by the disk.
  • Ordering 6, inner to outer $dy$, $dx$, $dz$: $$ \int_{-3}^{3}\int_{-2}^{2}\int_{-\sqrt{9-z^2}}^{\sqrt{9-z^2}} dy\,dx\,dz $$ The $z$ range is fixed by the disk.

Notes

The two planes keep $y$ and $z$; the cylinder keeps $x$ free.

The disk symmetry in $y$ and $z$ explains the paired forms.
