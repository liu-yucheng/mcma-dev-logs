15.7.50: mass, center of mass, and moment of inertia of a cut cylinder

E is the solid in the first octant bounded by the cylinder $y^2 + z^2 = 9$ and the planes $x = 0$, $y = 3x$, $z = 0$. Its density is $\rho(x, y, z) = x^2 + y^2$.

Part (a): the mass

Step 1.1: the volume in cylindrical coordinates

The cylinder reads $r \leq 3$; the wall $y = 3x$ reads $\tan\theta = 1/3$, that is $\theta = \pi/6$.

$$ V = \int_0^{\pi/6}\int_0^3 r\,dr\,d\theta + \int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}\int_0^{3\sin\theta} r\,dr\,d\theta = \frac{9\pi}{2} $$

The volume is $\frac{9\pi}{2}$, about 14.137, printed by part1_step1.py.

part1_step1.py compares the closed form with a grid count of the same inequalities.

The wall is $y = 3x$, not $x = 3y$; it cuts off the sector $0 \leq \theta \leq \pi/6$.

Step 1.2: the mass is $19\pi$

In cylindrical form $\rho = r^2$, so the mass integral carries $r^3$.

$$ M = \int_0^{\pi/6}\int_0^3 r^3\,dr\,d\theta + \int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}\int_0^{3\sin\theta} r^3\,dr\,d\theta = \frac{729\pi}{8} + \frac{81\pi}{8} = 19\pi $$

The mass is $19\pi$, about 59.690, printed by part1_step2.py.

part1_step2.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo rejection sample.

A reader may forget the Jacobian $r$ and integrate $r^2$ instead of $r^3$.

Part (b): the center of mass

Step 2.1: the three first moments

The first moments are the triple integrals of $x\rho$, $y\rho$, and $z\rho$.

$$ \begin{aligned} M_{yz} &= \frac{1215\sqrt{3}}{32} + \frac{729\pi}{16} \\ M_{xz} &= \frac{M_{yz}}{3} \\ M_z &= \frac{243}{19} \end{aligned} $$

The center of mass is $\left(\frac{M_{yz}}{19\pi},\, \frac{M_{xz}}{19\pi},\, \frac{4}{3}\right)$, about $(1.494, 0.747, 0.632)$, printed by part2_step1.py.

part2_step1.py compares each coordinate with a weighted Monte Carlo mean.

The ratio $\bar{x}/\bar{y} = 3$ follows from the wall; a wrong wall inverts it.

Part (c): the moment of inertia

Step 3.1: the moment of inertia about the $z$-axis

The radius to the $z$-axis is $r$, so the integrand is $r^2\rho$ times the Jacobian $r$.

$$ I_z = \int_0^{\pi/6}\int_0^3 r^5\,dr\,d\theta + \int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}\int_0^{3\sin\theta} r^5\,dr\,d\theta = \frac{1215\pi}{56} + \frac{1215\pi}{56} = \frac{2430\pi}{7} $$

The moment of inertia is $\frac{2430\pi}{7}$, about 1089.568, printed by part3_step1.py.

part3_step1.py compares the closed form with a Monte Carlo sample of $r^4\rho$.

The power of $r$ is five, not four; the radius contributes one more factor.

Answer

The mass is $19\pi$ about 59.690, the center of mass is about $(1.494, 0.747, 0.632)$, and $I_z$ is $\frac{2430\pi}{7}$ about 1089.568.

Notes

  • Both integrals use the cylindrical pair $r, \theta$.
  • The wall $y = 3x$ opens at $\theta = \pi/6$.
  • Every value is printed by a script named in its step.
