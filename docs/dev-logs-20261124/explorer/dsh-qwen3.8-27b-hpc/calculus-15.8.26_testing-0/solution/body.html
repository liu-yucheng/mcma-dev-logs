15.8.26 Volume Cut Out by the Tilted Cylinder

Use cylindrical coordinates to find the volume of the solid that the cylinder $r = a\cos\theta$ cuts out of the sphere of radius $a$ centered at the origin. Illustrate the solid by graphing the sphere and the cylinder on the same screen.
ItemContent
GivenThe sphere $r^2 + z^2 \leq a^2$ and the cylinder $r = a\cos\theta$, with $a > 0$.
FindThe volume $V$ of the solid cut out, and a graph of both surfaces.
SymbolMeaningUnit
$a$Radius of the sphere and scale of the cylinderlength
$r, \theta, z$Cylindrical coordinates of a pointlength, angle
$D$The disk $r \leq a\cos\theta$ in the plane $z = 0$area
$V$Volume of the solid cut outvolume

Part 1: The volume in cylindrical coordinates

Step 1.1: Integrate the height over r

The sphere is $r^2 + z^2 \leq a^2$, so the height above $(r, \theta)$ is $2\sqrt{a^2 - r^2}$. The disk $D$ has $0 \leq r \leq a\cos\theta$ and $-\frac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$, and it lies inside the circle $r \leq a$.

$$V = \int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} \int_0^{a\cos\theta} 2r\sqrt{a^2 - r^2} \, dr \, d\theta = \int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} \frac{2a^3}{3}\left(1 - |\sin\theta|^3\right) d\theta$$

The inner integral is $\frac{2a^3}{3}(1 - |\sin\theta|^3)$ at every $\theta$.

The script part1_step1.py checks the exact inner integral against a trapezoid grid, tolerance $10^{-4}$.

A common slip forgets the Jacobian $r$ and integrates the height alone.

Step 1.2: Integrate over theta

The integrand $1 - |\sin\theta|^3$ is even. Halve the range, drop the absolute value, and close the integral.

$$V = \frac{4a^3}{3} \int_0^{\pi/2} (1 - \sin^3\theta) \, d\theta = \frac{4a^3}{3}\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{2}{3}\right) = \frac{6\pi - 8}{9}\,a^3$$

The volume is $V = \frac{6\pi - 8}{9}a^3 \approx 1.2055\,a^3$.

The script part1_step2.py asserts a Monte Carlo volume against the exact value, tolerance $0.02$.

A common slip keeps $\sin\theta$ signed over the full range and loses the $\frac{8}{9}a^3$ cut.

Part 2: The picture

Step 2.1: The intersection curve

On the cylinder, $r = a\cos\theta$. On the sphere, $r^2 + z^2 = a^2$. Together they give $z^2 = a^2\sin^2\theta$, the Viviani curve.

$$\gamma(\theta) = (a\cos^2\theta, \, a\cos\theta\sin\theta, \, a\sin\theta), \qquad -\frac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$$

The upper and lower branches meet at $(a, 0, 0)$ and at the origin.

The script part2_step1.py checks a grid of curve points against both surface equations, tolerance $10^{-12}$.

A common slip drops the factor $\cos\theta$ from $y$ and plots a circle instead of the figure-eight curve.

Step 2.2: The section boundary at three heights

At height $z$ the section keeps the points with $r \leq a\cos\theta$ and $r \leq \sqrt{a^2 - z^2}$. The boundary radius is the smaller of the two.

$$r_{\max}(z, \theta) = \min\left(a\cos\theta, \sqrt{a^2 - z^2}\right), \qquad -\frac{\pi}{2} \leq \theta \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$$

The boundary at $z = 0$ and $z = \pm\frac{1}{2}$ prints with the corner list at each $\theta$ endpoint, for $a = 1$.

The script part2_step2.py compares a Monte Carlo section area with a trapezoid of the exact boundary, tolerance $5 \times 10^{-3}$.

A common slip uses only the cylinder radius $a\cos\theta$ and ignores the sphere cap at the top heights.

Answer

The volume is $V = \frac{6\pi - 8}{9}\,a^3 = \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{8}{9}\right)a^3 \approx 1.2055\,a^3$.

Notes

  • The cylinder disk lies inside the sphere footprint, since $r \leq a\cos\theta \leq a$.
  • The curve in Figure 1 is the Viviani curve, one loop for the upper branch.
